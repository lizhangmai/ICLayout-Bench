These use the upstream RF testbench conventions and same-condition pre-layout observations. High-frequency absolute gain retains delivered signal in the objective, so attenuation cannot improve matching without a cost. Model scope is upstream kpex 2.5D CC plus native devices; inductance/full-wave accuracy is not claimed by the scoring extraction. Each scored RF metric uses one-sided degradation q=budget/(budget+loss), with q=1 for an improvement. Budgets are 0.5 dB for delivered gain and input reflection, and 1 dB for output reflection. A 0.5 dB gain budget represents about 5.6% amplitude loss. The input matching budget resolves the upstream physical design's roughly 1 dB return-loss margin; the broader output budget resolves a 1 dB loss within the source's roughly 4 dB margin. They set quality sensitivity, not functional cutoffs, and exceed numerical measurement noise. Ordinary db20 conversion would credit a 1 dB reflection degradation at about 0.89, obscuring these margins even at high weight."
