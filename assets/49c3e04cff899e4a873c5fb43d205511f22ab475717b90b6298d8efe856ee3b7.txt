# Self-Biased LDO Reference Amplifier Layout Task

## Objective

Implement `amp_033_ti_ldo_ref_selfbias` as a GF180MCU D layout. Nine MOS devices form the fixed upstream two-stage Miller amplifier, with a matched PMOS mirror and diode-connected self-bias. External unity feedback tests the reference-buffer function; no ideal internal amplifier or bias servo is inserted.

## Inputs and Interface

`materials/circuit.cdl` is the authoritative physical circuit, `materials/circuit.spice` its native simulation-model wrapper, and `materials/testbench.spice` defines all measurements. Ordered ports are `vdd vout vinp vinn vss`, respectively supply, output, differential positive/negative inputs and ground; no external bias current is needed. Preserve connectivity and total device sizes; equivalent parallel fingering is allowed, but the amplifier topology must not be replaced.

## Operating Conditions

Typical native GF180 6 V MOS models, 3.3 V supply, 27 C, statistics disabled.
Input common mode is 0.8 V. Both 1 pF and 10 pF output loads are evaluated.
DC uses unity feedback. AC uses a 1 TH feedback inductor and 1 F input shunt
from 1 Hz to 1 GHz, 100 points/decade. Transient replaces these fixture elements
with 1 pH and 1 aF: the input rises 10 mV at 1 us and falls at 3 us, each in
10 ns. The simulation ends at 5 us, with a 1 ns requested step.
These ideal fixture elements are external test apparatus, outside the DUT.

## Electrical Requirements and Scoring

Physical checks and the declared functional bounds must pass. Quality scoring imposes no fixed degradation allowance. Each `source_*` job and its corresponding candidate extracted circuit use exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters, loads and measurement windows. Independent source observations define the 100-point electrical baseline without depending on the submitted GDS; reports retain every paired result.

Let x be the post-layout measurement and b the source-circuit measurement under the same conditions:

- Ratio normalization: maximize with q = x/b and minimize with q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared, use (x+s)/(b+s) and its reciprocal, respectively. The scale handles zero values as a normalization floor; it is neither an allowed degradation nor an acceptance bound.
- Amplitude in dB: maximize with q = 10^((x-b)/20) and minimize with its reciprocal.
- Target normalization: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), where s is the declared physical scale. Signed or zero-valued operating points are not divided directly.

Use `layout` scoring. Each metric takes its worst paired quality q, and the total score is S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), with area quality q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to 1; metric dimensions describe measurements and do not determine weights. Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements leave the score unknown, and zero-weight observations must also be valid. Matching source-circuit performance at the reference area scores 100 points; better valid solutions may exceed 100. Weights express quality tradeoffs rather than hard bounds.

`tracking_error_v` uses `saturating_ratio`: q = 2(b+s)/(b+x+2s), where x is candidate error, b is paired source error, and s is the positive scale below. Source-equivalent error gives q=1; improvement approaches the upper limit 2 with diminishing returns. s retains the declared units and numeric-floor meaning. Normalize each condition before taking the worst quality; other normalization and functional rules follow their own declarations.

DC output must lie between the supply rails; power, absolute tracking error and signed output-step observations must be nonnegative, with zero allowed. These gates express physical domains and buffer function. Required measurements must be finite; missing or failed measurements are evaluation errors rather than low performance. Upstream datasheet targets are not gates; phase margin supports continuous comparison without upstream thresholds.

Pair each quality observation with independent source simulation using exactly the same deck, load, supply, temperature and models. Output target scale is 3.3 V and phase scale 180 degrees; the power-ratio floor 1 pW and tracking-error floor 1 mV are normalization units, not tolerances.

Area target: **6223.21 um2**. Device/contact envelopes sum to 3808.978200 um2,
using `(W+2)*(L+2)` per expanded MOS, resistor segment and capacitor tile
in micrometers. The estimate adds 50% routing plus a 2 um outer margin:

This engineering anchor is independent of the witness footprint and is not a
foundry minimum. Coefficient **6** reflects a complete compensated two-stage
