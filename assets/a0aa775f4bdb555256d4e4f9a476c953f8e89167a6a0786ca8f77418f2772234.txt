# Tan CLIA Three-Stage Amplifier Layout Task

## Objective

Implement `amp_017_tan_clia` with the supplied fixed topology and dimensions. The PMOS input pair and cascoded NMOS branches drive voutn/net050. XM70 and XM71/XM72 form the current-inversion path through net3/net5; XM61 and the DM_1-controlled XM73 bias this path. XM68/XM69 drive the output. The 889198 ohm resistor from net5 to net2, 0.61268 pF net2-to-vss capacitor and 1.07905 pF net8-to-vout capacitor all remain.
Do not replace the circuit, delete compensation, or add a servo. The physical core
contains 512 MOS after expanding explicit upstream multiplicities. Each PMOS
input body remains tied to its own tail node through its physical well contact.

## Inputs and Interface

`materials/circuit.cdl` is the authoritative physical circuit, `materials/circuit.spice` its simulation representation, `materials/testbench.spice` defines closed-loop observations, and `materials/ac.spice` defines balanced differential AC excitation. Ordered ports are `vss vdd vinn vinp vout net1`: ground, supply, negative/positive inputs, single-ended output and an external bias-current pull-down. Bias, input drive, feedback connections and loads are external test fixtures. Contacts and internal R/C must be physically implemented.

## Operating Conditions

GF180MCU D, nominal TT/typical, 27 C, supply 1.8 V, input reference 0.3 V,
external current sink 2.32803e-06 A to vss, and 10 pF output load. Use the fixed GF180 3.3 V device binding at its authored 1.8 V operating rail; round W/L to the 5 nm manufacturing grid.
The closed-loop deck ties vinn directly to vout, drives vinp with a +100 mV pulse
starting at 20 us (100 ns rise/fall, 80 us high duration), and runs to 200 us with
10 ns maximum steps, Gear order 2, reltol=1e-5, abstol=1 pA, vntol=10 nV.
Transient starts from the solved DC point; this is not a zero-state supply-ramp
startup test. High and return windows are 80–100 and 180–200 us.

AC sweeps 200 points per decade from 1 Hz to 1 GHz. The differential-mode deck retains output DC feedback through a 1 TH inductor and isolates the bias node from AC through a 1 F capacitor to ground; vinp/vinn use balanced +0.5/-0.5 V small-signal excitation. Input common-mode residual amplitude and DC feedback error must both be <=1 uV. These are external small-signal bias fixtures, not physical compensation; an independent closed-loop deck measures actual follower response and does not call a voltage-transfer estimate a loop return ratio.

## Physical Requirements

Supply a GDS <=64 MiB with top cell `amp_017_tan_clia`, within 20000 by 2000 um
(the task's artifact-size/outline bounds). Native DRC, strict named-interface LVS
and functional-area measurement must pass; no marker waivers apply. Preserve
MOS dimensions, multiplicities, body connections, all physical R/C and tap geometry.
Functional layers in the published task include active, implant, wells, gates,
contacts, passive plates, vias and routing; annotation/text-only layers are excluded.
GF180 native standalone-block DRC includes FEOL, BEOL, connectivity, manufacturing grid, antenna and dummy fill, with chip-level density and seal-ring closure outside scope. Native MIM-B uses M4/M5 and 2 fF/um2; resistors use 1 kohm/square unsilicided poly. The model boundary excludes distributed substrate resistance. Candidate-derived Magic distributed RC feeds every post-layout job.

## Electrical Requirements and Scoring

Physical checks and the declared functional bounds must pass. Quality scoring imposes no fixed degradation allowance. Each `source_*` job and its corresponding candidate extracted circuit use exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters, loads and measurement windows. Independent source observations define the 100-point electrical baseline without depending on the submitted GDS; reports retain every paired result.

Let x be the post-layout measurement and b the source-circuit measurement under the same conditions:

- Ratio normalization: maximize with q = x/b and minimize with q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared, use (x+s)/(b+s) and its reciprocal, respectively. The scale handles zero values as a normalization floor; it is neither an allowed degradation nor an acceptance bound.
- Amplitude in dB: maximize with q = 10^((x-b)/20) and minimize with its reciprocal.
- Target normalization: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), where s is the declared physical scale. Signed or zero-valued operating points are not divided directly.

Use `layout` scoring. Each metric takes its worst paired quality q, and the total score is S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), with area quality q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to 1; metric dimensions describe measurements and do not determine weights. Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements leave the score unknown, and zero-weight observations must also be valid. Matching source-circuit performance at the reference area scores 100 points; better valid solutions may exceed 100. Weights express quality tradeoffs rather than hard bounds.

`late_error_v`, `return_error_v`, `ripple_v` and `return_ripple_v` use `saturating_ratio`. Candidate error x, paired source error b and the positive scale s below define q = 2(b+s)/(b+x+2s). Source-equivalent error gives q=1; decreasing error approaches the upper quality limit 2 with diminishing returns. The scale retains its declared unit and numerical-floor meaning. Normalize each condition before taking the worst paired quality; other metrics retain their declared normalization and functional requirements.

All measurements must be finite and complete. Supply power, absolute errors and amplitude ranges must obey their nonnegative physical definitions. The common-mode test-apparatus bound validates differential excitation, rather than DUT common-mode regulation. The DC feedback bound excludes failure of the isolated bias solve. Both apparatus checks apply to source and candidate simulation before scoring. These are this task's hard electrical requirements; upstream paper targets are not acceptance bounds. Window error and ripple do not establish settling or periodic steady state; no phase margin, unity-gain crossing, settling time, CMRR, noise, PVT or mismatch performance is claimed.

Quality uses independently simulated same-condition source observations. For gain,
q=10^((candidate−source)/20); target q=1/(1+abs(candidate−source)/scale);
inverse-ratio q=(source+floor)/(candidate+floor). The 6 dB target scale corresponds
to a factor-of-two amplitude change; 1.8 V is the bias normalization and 1 mV is
1% of the 100 mV test step, preventing numerical zero error from dominating quality.
Area quality is Q=88555.19 um2/functional_area.

The compact-area anchor is independent of any reference layout: sum
m*((W+2.4)*(L+2.4)+3.2^2) um2 over upstream-bound MOS, add 1.2*C/density
for capacitors (density 2 fF/um2) and 2*R/sheet_resistance um2
for resistor contact/routing envelopes (sheet resistance 1000 ohm),
then apply a 50% global routing allowance. This is an engineering estimate, not a
foundry minimum. Coefficient 7: Multistage compensation includes a resistively isolated capacitor and a current-inversion output-drive path.
