Layout-Bench modifications to INV_X1, NOR2_X1, and XOR2_X1
---------------------------------------------------------
The SPICE files are retained from the pinned upstream publication without changes.
Their GDS reference layouts were modified for this collection: explicit well and
substrate taps were added at the right side, supply rails and poly ends were
extended, and the supply labels were moved inside the rails. These changes use
the same geometry pattern as the collection's qualified NAND2_X1 reference.
The original GDS files are at the matching pinned paths in
https://foss-eda-tools.googlesource.com/third_party/freepdk45/+/356e90646f5ef26ea09b1ed8ce4796871403a0c7/Back_End/gds/.
