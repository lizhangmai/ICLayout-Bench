# StrongARM Dynamic Comparator Layout Task

## Objective

Implement `cmp_002_strongarm` as a GF180MCU D layout. Fifteen upstream MOS instances implement the clocked StrongARM latch: differential input pair, clocked tail, cross-coupled regenerative devices, four precharge devices and two output inverters. The input pair multiplicities expand the physical circuit to seventeen devices. The maintained GF180 3.3 V binding uses the fixed sizing defaults and authored ties from the pinned accession.

## Inputs and Interface

`materials/circuit.cdl` is the physical authority; `materials/circuit.spice`
contains matching native simulator wrappers. `materials/testbench.spice` defines
all measurements. Ordered ports: `vdd voutp voutn vinp vinn clk vss`.

`vdd/vss`: supply and return; `vinp/vinn`: differential inputs; `clk`: evaluate high, precharge low; `voutp/voutn`: buffered differential outputs. Positive input difference produces positive output difference. Supply, clock, input drivers and output loads are external apparatus. No offset-trim variant or proprietary process deck is included.

## Operating Conditions

Typical GF180 3.3 V MOS models at 3.3 V supply and 27 C, statistics disabled. Differential input is +20 or -20 mV at common modes 0.9, 1.2, 1.65 and 2.1 V. Each output has a 50 fF load. The 50 MHz clock first rises at 10 ns, has 100 ps or 500 ps rise/fall edges and 10 ns pulse width. Simulate to 100 ns at 10 ps requested steps; timing uses the fifth evaluation edge near 90 ns. All 16 common-mode, input-polarity and edge-rate combinations are checked, with scored metrics aggregated at their worst paired result.

Native standalone DRC covers geometry, connectivity, manufacturing grid, dummy
fill and antenna; chip-level density and seal-ring closure are excluded.
LVS must match all devices, parameters and named ports. Metal1 labels identify
ports. The functional bounding rectangle includes the device, well, implant,
contact, passive and complete routing layers enumerated in the runtime outline;
text annotations are excluded. Maximum outline is 5000 by 1000 um.

Keep the 3.3 V models, 0.28 um channel length, fixed widths and multiplicities. Parallel equivalent fingers are permitted. All MOS dimensions lie on the native 5 nm construction grid. Body/well connections are explicit, with native guard-ring/tap geometry. The IHP binding has the same topology but different process sizing; it is not mixed into this GF180 task.

Candidate-derived native Magic coupling capacitance and distributed wire
resistance feed post-layout simulation, with zero omission thresholds.
Physical MOS diffusion areas/perimeters come from the layout. Substrate/well
connections are ideal: distributed substrate resistance/noise and inductance
are not represented. Sixteen nominal input-polarity/common-mode/clock-edge combinations, reset, decision delay/slew and average supply power. Coefficient 6 reflects regenerative decisions with clocked reset and buffered loads. Offset distribution, metastability statistics, noise, PVT and RF/EM are outside this declared scope.

## Electrical Requirements and Scoring

Physical checks and the declared functional bounds must pass. Quality scoring imposes no fixed degradation allowance. Each `source_*` job and its corresponding candidate extracted circuit use exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters, loads and measurement windows. Independent source observations remain required evidence. The decision-delay quality target is 700 ps; other electrical metrics retain their source anchors. Reports retain every paired result.

Let x be the post-layout measurement and b the declared quality target, or the same-condition source measurement when no target is declared:

- Ratio normalization: maximize with q = x/b and minimize with q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared, use (x+s)/(b+s) and its reciprocal, respectively. The scale handles zero values as a normalization floor; it is neither an allowed degradation nor an acceptance bound.
- Amplitude in dB: maximize with q = 10^((x-b)/20) and minimize with its reciprocal.
- Target normalization: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), where s is the declared physical scale. Signed or zero-valued operating points are not divided directly.

Use `layout` scoring. First compute each metric's raw quality q from the worst paired condition, with raw area quality q_area = area_target / candidate_functional_area. Credit c_i = min(1, q_i) for every positively weighted metric, then compute S = 100 * product(c_i ** w_i). The weights sum to 1 and the score is bounded by 100. An improvement beyond one target cannot compensate for missing another target. Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements leave the score unknown, and zero-weight observations must also be valid. Reaching every positively weighted target scores 100; a feasible reference may score far below 100. Weights express quality tradeoffs, not acceptance bounds.

| Metric | Definition | Unit | Dimension | Normalization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `delay_s` | Clock 1.65 V crossing to signed output difference 1.65 V, fifth rising edge | s | response | ratio |
| `slew_s` | Signed differential output rise from 0.33 to 2.97 V, fifth decision | s | response | ratio |
| `power_w` | Mean supply power over 20–100 ns | W | supply | ratio |
| `decision_v` | Input-polarity-adjusted output difference at 99 ns | V | bias | target |
| `reset_p_v` | Positive output at 89 ns in precharge | V | functional | unscored |
| `reset_n_v` | Negative output at 89 ns in precharge | V | functional | unscored |

Signed decision must be 1.65–3.3 V; both reset outputs must be 0–1.65 V. Half-supply separates the required resolved/reset logic states, independent of any upstream speed target. Delay, slew and mean supply power must be nonnegative. Timing has no hard upper bound beyond the declared observation window; a missing crossing is an invalid measurement.
Every condition must complete; missing, nonfinite or wrong-unit observations
produce an evaluation error. Upstream advertised performance is not a
qualification requirement.

Scored observations pair with independent source jobs using the identical
stimuli, parameters, models and backend. Target scales are 3.3 V; ratio floors
use 1 pW for power. These are normalization units, not allowed degradation.
Delay/slew ratios have no floor.

The area scoring target is **100 um2**; the decision-delay target is **700 ps** at the stated loads and conditions. Neither is an additional acceptance limit.

Worst decision delay receives 55% because regenerative-node parasitics and branch routing directly affect the 16-condition decision sweep. A 100 um2 footprint receives 30%, power 10% and slew 5%. Rail decision amplitude remains checked but is not repeatedly rewarded. The 700 ps target is just beyond the slowest source decision (about 671 ps), and is independent of the much looser functional observation window.

Capability coefficient **6** is independent of the quality score.
