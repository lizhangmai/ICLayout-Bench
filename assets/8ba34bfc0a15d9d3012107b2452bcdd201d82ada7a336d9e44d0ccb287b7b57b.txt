# FreePDK45 INV_X1 Layout Task

## Objective

Implement the fixed-size transistor inverter `INV_X1` in FreePDK45. Preserve ordered ports A ZN VDD VSS, four-terminal connections, VTL models and fixed sizes, implementing A=0→ZN=1, A=1→ZN=0. Solve budget: **3 hours**.

## Inputs and Interface

Declared inputs are this description, `materials/circuit.spice` and `materials/testbench.spice`. The evaluation environment supplies model, extraction, DRC and LVS resources; reference GDS and qualification evidence are maintainer-only.

## Operating Conditions

Nominal FreePDK45 BSIM4, 1.0 V, 27 °C, ZN load 5 fF. Nine 500 ps slots drive A=0,1,0,1,0,1,0,1,0 with 10 ps edges; sample +110 ps after each slot starts. Transient ends at 4.5 ns with 0.5 ps sampling steps; average supply power over 0–4.4 ns. Only this nominal condition is scored.

## Physical Requirements

Submit nonempty `output/final.gds` with top cell `INV_X1`, at most 10 MiB. Pass FreePDK45 KLayout DRC and strict named-port LVS without waivers, checking body terminals, MOS models and W/L. Research rules do not constitute manufacturing signoff.

The functional bounding-box width and height on drawing layers 1/0–29/0 must each be at most 100 µm. The standard-cell frame has fixed height 1.63 µm and width on a 0.01 µm grid. Relative to its lower-left corner, continuous metal1 VDD/VSS rails have centers at y=1.515/0.115 µm and connect to the corresponding ports; thickness is at least 0.07 µm, extending from x=0.115 µm to the right boundary minus 0.030 µm. Electrical labels lie on poly or metal drawing layers. Global translation, equivalent hierarchy, internal renaming and source/drain exchange are allowed if all physical and electrical checks pass.

## Electrical Requirements and Scoring

At every sample, ZN must satisfy the expected CMOS logic level: high at least 0.9 V, low at most 0.1 V.

Measure each of the 8 input edges that change the output: propagation delay runs from the input 0.5 V crossing to the output 0.5 V crossing; output rise transition is 0.2→0.8 V and fall transition is 0.8→0.2 V. Independent source simulation with the same testbench defines electrical baselines; compare delay and transition per edge, taking the worst source/candidate quality ratio. Average supply power is paired with the source using a 1 pW normalization floor.

The functional-area target is 1.095360 µm², 80% of the verified reference area 1.369200 µm², and is only a quality target. q_area=target/candidate area; q_delay is the minimum source/candidate delay ratio over 8 edges, q_transition is the minimum source/candidate transition-time ratio, and q_supply is the source/candidate average-power ratio with a 1 pW floor.

| Metric | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| `functional_area` | 0.2 |
| `propagation_delay` | 0.48 |
| `output_transition` | 0.16 |
| `supply` | 0.16 |

Supply quality q_supply = (P_source + 1 pW)/(P_candidate + 1 pW), using the declared 1 pW normalization offset. Total score is 100 × q_area^0.20 × q_delay^0.48 × q_transition^0.16 × q_supply^0.16; propagation delay, output transition, power and area weights are 48%, 16%, 16%, 20%, respectively. Area at target with source-equivalent electrical behavior scores 100; better valid layouts may exceed 100. Physical or functional failure scores zero; after validity checks, there are no additional timing, power or area performance rejection thresholds.

## Tools and Submission

Use declared FreePDK45 KLayout DRC/LVS, Magic RC and ngspice models, and submit only `output/final.gds` with top cell `INV_X1`. Coverage is limited to nominal tests and supply-rail geometry, excluding cell-array integration, PVT, mismatch and manufacturing signoff.
