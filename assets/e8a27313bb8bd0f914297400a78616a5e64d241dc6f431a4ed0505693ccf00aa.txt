# Ramos PFC Three-Stage Amplifier Layout Task

## Objective

Implement `amp_014_ramos_pfc` with the supplied fixed topology and dimensions. The PMOS input pair drives the cascoded NMOS branches at dm_2/net063; the voutn mirror produces net050. XM10 and the XM21/XM22 mirror drive net049, while XM11/XM23 drive the single-ended output. Both compensation paths remain: 63 pF from net050 to vout and 25 pF from net050 to net049. The vb3/vb4 bias network is retained.
Do not replace the circuit, delete compensation, or add a servo. The physical core
contains 2583 MOS after expanding explicit upstream multiplicities. Each PMOS
input body remains tied to its own tail node through its physical well contact.

## Inputs and Interface

`materials/circuit.cdl` is the physical authority; `materials/circuit.spice` is its
matching simulator representation. `materials/testbench.spice` defines closed-loop
observations and `materials/ac.spice` defines balanced differential AC excitation.
Ordered ports: `vss vdd vinn vinp vout net1`. `vss` is ground, `vdd` supply,
`vinn/vinp` negative/positive input, `vout` the single-ended output, and `net1`
the external bias-current sink. Bias, input drivers, feedback wiring and load are
external test apparatus; taps and all internal R/C are physical DUT elements.

## Operating Conditions

IHP SG13G2, nominal TT/typical, 27 C, supply 1.5 V, input reference 0.4 V,
external current sink 2.4e-05 A to vss, and 10 pF output load. Use the fixed IHP LV binding at 1.5 V, adapting the authored 1.8 V stimulus to the LV process rail; retain its bias current and input reference. Round W/L to 5 nm (the supplied integer-micrometre sizes are unchanged).
The closed-loop deck ties vinn directly to vout, drives vinp with a +100 mV pulse
starting at 20 us (100 ns rise/fall, 80 us high duration), and runs to 200 us with
10 ns maximum steps, Gear order 2, reltol=1e-5, abstol=1 pA, vntol=10 nV.
Transient starts from the solved DC point; this is not a zero-state supply-ramp
startup test. High and return windows are 80–100 and 180–200 us.

AC spans 1 Hz–1 GHz at 200 points/decade. The differential deck retains DC output
feedback through a 1 TH inductor and AC-isolates its bias node with 1 F to ground;
vinp/vinn receive balanced +0.5/-0.5 V small-signal excitation. It verifies residual
input common-mode amplitude <=1 uV and DC feedback mismatch <=1 uV. These are external small-signal bias fixtures,
not physical compensation. The separate closed-loop deck measures actual follower
response. No voltage-transfer estimate is labeled a loop return ratio.

## Physical Requirements

Supply a GDS <=64 MiB with top cell `amp_014_ramos_pfc`, within 20000 by 2000 um
(the task's artifact-size/outline bounds). Native DRC, strict named-interface LVS
and functional-area measurement must pass; no marker waivers apply. Preserve
MOS dimensions, multiplicities, body connections, all physical R/C and tap geometry.
Functional layers in the published task include active, implant, wells, gates,
contacts, passive plates, vias and routing; annotation/text-only layers are excluded.
IHP native main and maximal DRC use the pinned PDK block profiles, with chip-level density and antenna disabled. Native MIM and high-poly primitives retain their compact models. Magic idealizes well/substrate taps; source simulation retains the explicit finite tap models. This is not distributed substrate or RF/EM extraction. Candidate-derived Magic distributed RC feeds every post-layout job.

## Electrical Requirements and Scoring

Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each `source_*` job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.

For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:

- Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
  use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
  s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.
- Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.
- Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
  zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.

Scoring uses `layout`. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.

| Metric | Definition | Unit | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gain_db, gain_10khz_db | Balanced differential Vout/(Vinp−Vinn) magnitude at 1 Hz and 10 kHz | dB | Response, source-paired db20 maximize |
| closed_gain_10khz_db | Actual unity-follower transfer at 10 kHz | dB | Response target, 6 dB scale |
| output_v | DC follower output | V | Bias target, 1.5 V scale |
| power_w | DC power drawn from VDD | W | Supply, inverse ratio, 1 pW floor |
| mean_power_w | Mean supply power over 20–200 us | W | Supply, inverse ratio, 1 pW floor |
| late_error_v, return_error_v | Mean absolute tracking error in high/return window | V | Response, inverse ratio, 1 mV floor |
| ripple_v, return_ripple_v | Full output peak-to-peak range in high/return window | V | Response, inverse ratio, 1 mV floor |
| high_v, low_v | Window means | V | Diagnostic |
| bias_v | External bias port DC voltage | V | Diagnostic |
| output_min_v, output_max_v | Full-transient extrema | V | Diagnostic |
| dc_feedback_error_v | Absolute DC voltage across the external feedback inductor | V | Measurement-validity check, 0–1 uV |
| fixture_cm_max | Maximum residual common-mode AC drive | V | Measurement-validity check, 0–1 uV |

All measurements must be finite and complete. Nonnegative supply power and
absolute-error/range values follow their physical domains. The common-mode fixture
bound verifies the differential excitation, not DUT common-mode regulation. The
DC feedback bound rejects a failed bias-isolation solve. Both checks fail the
source or candidate simulation before scoring. These
are the only electrical hard bounds; upstream paper targets are not gates.
Window errors and ripple do not assert settled operation or periodic steady state.
No phase margin, unity crossing, settling time, CMRR, noise, PVT or mismatch is claimed.

Quality uses independently simulated same-condition source observations. For gain,
q=10^((candidate−source)/20); target q=1/(1+abs(candidate−source)/scale);
inverse-ratio q=(source+floor)/(candidate+floor). The 6 dB target scale corresponds
to a factor-of-two amplitude change; 1.5 V is the bias normalization and 1 mV is
1% of the 100 mV test step, preventing numerical zero error from dominating quality.
Area quality is Q=190064.1 um2/functional_area.

The compact-area anchor is independent of any reference layout: sum
m*((W+2.4)*(L+2.4)+3.2^2) um2 over upstream-bound MOS, add 1.2*C/density
for capacitors (density 1.5 fF/um2) and 2*R/sheet_resistance um2
for resistor contact/routing envelopes (sheet resistance 1360 ohm),
then apply a 50% global routing allowance. This is an engineering estimate, not a
foundry minimum. Coefficient 7: Multistage compensation couples the first-stage node, current-inversion path and output response.
