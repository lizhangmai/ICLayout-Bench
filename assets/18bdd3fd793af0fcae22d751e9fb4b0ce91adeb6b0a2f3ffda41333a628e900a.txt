# Self-Biased LDO Reference Amplifier Layout Task

## Objective

Implement `amp_033_ti_ldo_ref_selfbias` as a GF180MCU D layout. Nine MOS devices form the fixed upstream two-stage Miller amplifier, with a matched PMOS mirror and diode-connected self-bias. External unity feedback tests the reference-buffer function; no ideal internal amplifier or bias servo is inserted.


## Inputs and Interface

`materials/circuit.cdl` is the physical authority. `materials/circuit.spice`
contains the corresponding native simulator wrappers; `materials/testbench.spice`
defines all measurements. Ordered ports: `vdd vout vinp vinn vss`.
`vdd/vss` are supply/return, `vinp/vinn` are differential inputs, and `vout` is the output.
No external bias current is required. Preserve connectivity and total device dimensions;
parallel equivalent fingers are permitted. Do not substitute a different amplifier.

## Operating Conditions

Typical native GF180 6 V MOS models, 3.3 V supply, 27 C, statistics disabled.
Input common mode is 0.8 V. Both 1 pF and 10 pF output loads are evaluated.
DC uses unity feedback. AC uses a 1 TH feedback inductor and 1 F input shunt
from 1 Hz to 1 GHz, 100 points/decade. Transient replaces these fixture elements
with 1 pH and 1 aF: the input rises 10 mV at 1 us and falls at 3 us, each in
10 ns. The simulation ends at 5 us, with a 1 ns requested step.
These ideal fixture elements are external test apparatus, outside the DUT.

## Physical Requirements

GDS database unit 0.001 um; maximum 10 MiB; top cell `amp_033_ti_ldo_ref_selfbias`.
Native standalone DRC includes geometry, connectivity, manufacturing grid,
dummy fill and antenna rules; chip density and seal-ring closure are excluded.
Native LVS must match devices, parameters and every named port. All device,
well, implant, contact, passive-marker and routing layers listed in the runtime
outline form the functional bounding rectangle; text annotations are excluded.
Maximum outline is 5000 by 1000 um. Labels belong on native Metal1 text.

The 6 V minimum channel lengths are 0.70 um NMOS and 0.55 um PMOS.
Upstream dimensions are rounded to 5 nm, with only subminimum lengths raised.
Physical passives use 1 kohm/square poly and MIM-B, 2 fF/um2, between M4/M5.
Long resistors are series segmented; capacitors are parallel tiled. Nominal
values are implemented by geometry; source and candidate use the same native
process models. MOS diffusion areas/perimeters and interconnect parasitics are
candidate-derived. Magic extracts nominal coupling capacitance and distributed
resistance using zero omission thresholds. Ideal well/substrate connections
exclude distributed substrate noise; no inductance, RF/EM or statistical claim
is made. Finite source and candidate observations must be available. Every declared
operating point must satisfy its functional checks; incomplete evaluation cannot
establish success.

## Electrical Requirements and Scoring

Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each `source_*` job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.

For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:

- Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
  use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
  s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.
- Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.
- Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
  zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.

Scoring uses `layout`. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.

Error quality uses `saturating_ratio` for `tracking_error_v`. For candidate error x, paired source error b and the positive scale s listed below, q = 2(b+s)/(b+x+2s). Source-equivalent error gives q=1; improvement approaches a quality ceiling of 2 with diminishing returns. The scale retains its declared units and numerical-floor meaning. Normalize each condition separately, then take the worst paired quality. Other normalizations and functional requirements retain their declared rules.

| Metric | Definition | Unit | Dimension | Normalization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `output_v` | DC output voltage | V | bias | target |
| `power_w` | DC supply power: -VDD*I(VDD) | W | supply | ratio |
| `gain_db` | Open-loop magnitude at 10 Hz in dB | dB | response | db20 |
| `unity_hz` | First falling unity-gain crossing | Hz | response | ratio |
| `phase_margin` | 180 degrees plus unwrapped phase at unity crossing | deg | response | target |
| `tracking_error_v` | Mean absolute unity-buffer tracking error over 1–5 us | V | response | saturating_ratio |
| `step_response_v` | Mean output at 2–3 us minus mean output at 4–5 us; nonnegative sign-domain check (zero is allowed) | V | functional | unscored |

DC output must remain between the rails, power and absolute tracking error must
be nonnegative, and the signed output-step observation must be nonnegative (zero is allowed). These
bounds express physical domains and buffer function. Every required measurement
must be finite; failed or missing measurements are evaluation errors, not poor
performance. No upstream data-sheet target is an acceptance gate. Phase margin
is measured and continuously compared, without importing an upstream threshold.

Each quality observation is paired with an independent source simulation using
the identical deck, load, supply, temperature and models. `target` uses scale
3.3 V for output and 180 degrees for phase; ratio floors are 1 pW for power
and 1 mV for tracking error. These are normalization units, not tolerances.


Area target: **6223.21 um2**. Device/contact envelopes sum to 3808.978200 um2,
using `(W+2)*(L+2)` per expanded MOS, resistor segment and capacitor tile
in micrometers. The estimate adds 50% routing plus a 2 um outer margin:
`ceil(100*(1.5*envelope_sum+8*sqrt(envelope_sum)+16))/100`.
This engineering anchor is independent of the witness footprint and is not a
foundry minimum. Coefficient **6** reflects a complete compensated two-stage
loop with self-bias and load-dependent transient response.
