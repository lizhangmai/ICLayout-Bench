Circuit materials derive from MacAnalog/spicexplorer-release analog-db,
commit 263d0322f8900dc331536fbbe6c0e804514fc454.
The database LICENSE and NOTICE identify original circuit definitions under
PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0; these terms are retained in LICENSE. The entry's
provenance metadata separately refers to an MIT source architecture; this
collection does not assume that metadata relicenses the normalized database
material. It is not covered as a whole by the framework's MIT license.

Layout-Bench changes: expose the reference and error-amplifier tail interfaces;
retain the GF180 MOS dimensions and pass-device multiplicity; replace the ideal
divider by GF180 1 kohm/square poly devices; remove the internal loop-probe source;
use a 0.9 V external reference for a nominal 1.8 V output; independently construct
the physical witness. Testbenches are separately identified as MIT.
Retain LICENSE and these notices with distributions of these materials.

Self-biased amplifiers, buffered LDO and StrongARM at the fixed snapshot

spicexplorer-analog-db
======================

This repository contains the SpiceXplorer analog circuit database (the canonical
topology/circuit registry + the tiered `verify` harness) and is part of the SpiceXplorer
platform. The harness code (`src/`) and the original circuit definitions are licensed under
the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 (see LICENSE), Copyright 2026 Danial Noori Zadeh.

Provenance of derived circuit artifacts
---------------------------------------
Some circuits are normalized from upstream open-source corpora. Their abstract netlists,
sizing, datasheets and schematic provenance are derived works; the upstream licenses apply to
the upstream material and are acknowledged here:

* circuits/amp_001_5t (formerly circuits/5t_ota) — derived from IIC-JKU / Harald Pretl
  `analog-circuit-design` (https://github.com/iic-jku/analog-circuit-design), Apache License 2.0.
  The datasheet is modeled on that project's CACE `voltage-buffer-ota.yaml`; the canonical
  schematic.svg and the xschem provenance are from that project.

* The **verifiable AnalogGym amplifier imports** — the 16 `amp_*` circuits whose `circuit.yaml`
  records `provenance.source: AnalogGym` (currently amp_002, amp_003, amp_005–amp_017 and amp_021)
  — normalized from CODA-Team `AnalogGym` (https://github.com/CODA-Team/AnalogGym), BSD 3-Clause
  License. These 3-stage amplifiers are lowered to open PDKs and simulated by this DB. This is
  **not** a contiguous id range — amp_004 is a CORA design and amp_018/amp_019 are original /
  template designs (see below). Reference snapshots of two un-imported AnalogGym amplifiers are
  kept at `_shared/references/analoggym/`.

* circuits/ldo_001_analoggym_basic, ldo_002_analoggym_folded_cascode, ldo_003_analoggym_simple —
  the **verifiable AnalogGym LDO imports**. ldo_001 normalizes AnalogGym's `Basic_LDO`
  (BSD 3-Clause); ldo_002 and ldo_003 pair AnalogGym testbenches + design-variable lists
  (BSD 3-Clause) with subcircuit netlists from `ChrisZonghaoLi/sky130_ldo_rl`
  (https://github.com/ChrisZonghaoLi/sky130_ldo_rl, Apache License 2.0). The full upstream→DB file
  mapping and both license texts are in corpora/analoggym-ldo/PROVENANCE.md.

* circuits/amp_004_folded_cascode (provenance.source: CORA), amp_018_telescopic_cascode,
  amp_019_ti_ldo_error, and ldo_004_basic_pmos / ldo_005_buffered_ref
  / ldo_006_stub / ldo_007_pmos — original first-party SpiceXplorer / CORA-benchmark designs
  (ldo_004/ldo_005 + amp_019 are
  first-party MacAnalog material modeled on a TI application-note LDO architecture, GF180-native,
  kept verbatim under each circuit's reference/). The NEWCAS 2026 research traces under
  circuits/amp_018_telescopic_cascode/artifacts/newcas2026/ are preserved verbatim.

* circuits/ferrosim_* (kind: reference, plan D-9) — POINTER entries for reference circuits from
  `Arcadia-1/ferrosim` (https://github.com/Arcadia-1/ferrosim), authored by Token Zhang, under the
  MIT License (some noted upstream as "ported from circuit-bench"). Each entry records upstream
  repository, designer, license, and classification ONLY — the upstream netlists and testbenches
  are NOT redistributed here, NOT lowered to an open PDK, and NOT simulated by this database.
  See corpora/ferrosim/README.md for the corpus-level pointer.

* circuits/sfe_* (kind: reference, plan D-9) — POINTER entries for the "Sensing Front End"
  category of CODA-Team `AnalogGym` (https://github.com/CODA-Team/AnalogGym), BSD 3-Clause
  License. Each entry records upstream provenance and classification ONLY — the upstream decks
  and testbenches are NOT redistributed here, NOT lowered to an open PDK, and NOT simulated by
  this database. The upstream→DB mapping is in corpora/analoggym-sensing-fe/PROVENANCE.md.

PDK corner libraries are NOT vendored here (plan D-6): circuits reference them by
`lib_file` + `section`; the database clones and runs its PDK-free tiers without any PDK.
----- END UPSTREAM analog-db/NOTICE -----
