11:T2395,<h1 id="problem-self-biased-ldo-error-amplifier-layout-task">Self-Biased LDO Error Amplifier Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>amp_032_ti_ldo_error_selfbias</code> as a GF180MCU D layout. Eleven MOS devices form the fixed upstream two-stage amplifier, with a level-shifted PMOS mirror, its 500 kohm gate-rail tie, diode-connected self-bias and series Miller compensation. The direct common-source output stage is the revision present in the pinned netlist; the retired stacked stage is not used.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code> is the physical authority. <code>materials/circuit.spice</code>
contains the corresponding native simulator wrappers; <code>materials/testbench.spice</code>
defines all measurements. Ordered ports: <code>vdd vout vinp vinn vss</code>.
<code>vdd/vss</code> are supply/return, <code>vinp/vinn</code> are differential inputs, and <code>vout</code> is the output.
No external bias current is required. Preserve connectivity and total device dimensions;
parallel equivalent fingers are permitted. Do not substitute a different amplifier.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Typical native GF180 6 V MOS models, 3.3 V supply, 27 C, statistics disabled.
Input common mode is 1.6 V. Both 1 pF and 10 pF output loads are evaluated.
DC uses unity feedback. AC uses a 1 TH feedback inductor and 1 F input shunt
from 1 Hz to 1 GHz, 100 points/decade. Transient replaces these fixture elements
with 1 pH and 1 aF: the input rises 10 mV at 1 us and falls at 3 us, each in
10 ns. The simulation ends at 5 us, with a 1 ns requested step.
These ideal fixture elements are external test apparatus, outside the DUT.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>GDS database unit 0.001 um; maximum 10 MiB; top cell <code>amp_032_ti_ldo_error_selfbias</code>.
Native standalone DRC includes geometry, connectivity, manufacturing grid,
dummy fill and antenna rules; chip density and seal-ring closure are excluded.
Native LVS must match devices, parameters and every named port. All device,
well, implant, contact, passive-marker and routing layers listed in the runtime
outline form the functional bounding rectangle; text annotations are excluded.
Maximum outline is 5000 by 1000 um. Labels belong on native Metal1 text.</p>
<p>The 6 V minimum channel lengths are 0.70 um NMOS and 0.55 um PMOS.
Upstream dimensions are rounded to 5 nm, with only subminimum lengths raised.
Physical passives use 1 kohm/square poly and MIM-B, 2 fF/um2, between M4/M5.
Long resistors are series segmented; capacitors are parallel tiled. Nominal
values are implemented by geometry; source and candidate use the same native
process models. MOS diffusion areas/perimeters and interconnect parasitics are
candidate-derived. Magic extracts nominal coupling capacitance and distributed
resistance using zero omission thresholds. Ideal well/substrate connections
exclude distributed substrate noise; no inductance, RF/EM or statistical claim
is made. Finite source and candidate observations must be available. Every declared
operating point must satisfy its functional checks; incomplete evaluation cannot
establish success.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each <code>source_*</code> job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.</p>
<p>For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.</li>
<li>Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.</li>
<li>Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Scoring uses <code>layout</code>. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.</p>
