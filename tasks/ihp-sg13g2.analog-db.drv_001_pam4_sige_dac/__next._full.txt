11:T2864,<h1 id="problem-broadband-sige-pam4-driver-layout-task">Broadband SiGe PAM4 Driver Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Lay out the upstream inductorless, three-cell current-steering PAM4 driver as
<code>pam4drv_pam4_lay</code>. One LSB cell and two parallel MSB cells contain twelve
three-finger <code>npn13G2</code> HBTs, twelve physical <code>rsil</code> resistors and three physical
MIM degeneration capacitors. Preserve the supplied topology and dimensions.
The task coefficient is 4.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code> is the native LVS authority;
<code>materials/circuit.spice</code> is its simulator representation;
<code>materials/testbench.spice</code> defines the electrical measurements.</p>
<p>Ordered ports: <code>lsbp lsbn msbp msbn outp outn vcc vcasc vcmb tmsb0 tlsb0 tmsb1 sub</code>.
The first four ports are differential LSB/MSB inputs, <code>outp/outn</code> are differential
outputs, <code>vcc</code> is the supply, <code>vcasc</code> biases the cascode bases, <code>vcmb</code> is the input
termination reference, the three tail ports connect to external current sinks,
and <code>sub</code> is the ideal substrate reference. The three testbench VCCS tails are
external bias apparatus, not missing physical transistors.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Use the upstream <strong>layout signoff point</strong>, not its separate static sizing point:
4 V supply, 3.35 V cascode bias, 1.9 V input common mode and 15 mA per cell.
The nominal corners are <code>hbt_typ</code>, <code>res_typ</code> and <code>cap_typ</code> at 27 °C. HBTs have
<code>Nx=3</code>. Physical dimensions are fixed by the netlists; the nominal targets are
50 Ω collector loads, 48 Ω input terminations, 3.2 Ω degeneration and 16 fF
bridging capacitance. PDK geometry models determine their actual values.</p>
<p>Both RF ports use 50 Ω per side (100 Ω differential). AC gain is differential
power-wave S21 = <code>2*Vout/Vsrc</code>, measured at 1 GHz; bandwidth is checked by the
50 GHz gain relative to 1 GHz. S11 is evaluated at <strong>32 GHz</strong>, and S22 at
<strong>50 GHz</strong>, using the testbench's 20 points/decade sweep and interpolation.
Do not substitute the preceding sweep samples for these endpoints.</p>
<p>The large-signal test drives LSB and MSB in phase at 1 GHz through the same
source impedances, with ±0.8 V per source around 1.9 V. Supplies and bias ramp
from zero over 8 ns; sine excitation starts at 9 ns. The 16–21 ns measurement
window contains five periods. Swing is twice the fundamental phasor amplitude
using the exported transient samples, rather than raw peak-to-peak excursion.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Submit GDS with the exact top-cell name, all thirteen labeled ports, and at most
10 MiB. Native SG13G2 DRC checks main and extra maximal rules without density or
antenna; no marker waivers apply. Native LVS compares devices, dimensions,
connectivity and named ports. Functional width/height must not exceed 160/120 µm.
The published <code>outline</code> layer list covers HBT, resistor, MIM, substrate-contact,
seven-metal and via geometry; pin/text and presentation layers are excluded.</p>
<p>Post-layout simulation uses KPEX 0.3.12 nominal 2.5D <strong>coupling capacitance</strong> from
the submitted GDS. Native extracted devices are checked against the candidate's
independent LVS database. MIM devices retain extracted endpoints and <code>w/l/m</code>;
no MIM layers are stripped and no source devices are reinserted. The process
reference plane is tied to <code>sub</code>. Distributed wire resistance, inductance,
pads/package, statistical yield and process-corner robustness are not claimed
by this upstream CC characterization contract. Finite substrate/tail-generator
circuits are outside the physical core. A PAM4 eye/RLM result is not established
by these tone and small-signal checks.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each <code>source_*</code> job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.</p>
<p>For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.</li>
<li>Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.</li>
<li>Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Scoring uses <code>layout</code>. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.</p>
<p>All measurements and their paired source observations must be finite and usable.
Performance is scored continuously against the same-condition source circuit;
upstream data-sheet targets are not acceptance thresholds. Supply consumption
and output swing must be nonnegative; zero or otherwise unusable ratio baselines
cannot establish a score. Physical checks remain mandatory.</p>
