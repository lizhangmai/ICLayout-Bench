11:T4d3a,<h1 id="problem-mirror-ota-regulator-with-bilateral-return-ratio-measurements-layout-task">Mirror-OTA Regulator with Bilateral Return-Ratio Measurements Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>ldo_008_fer_mirror_ota</code>: a mirror-loaded NMOS-input error amplifier,
diode/tail bias network, PMOS pass device, feedback divider and series R/C
compensation. Preserve all seven source MOS groups as 81 physical fingers,
40 MIM units and three high-poly resistors, with explicit substrate/well taps.
The internal compensation and divider remain part of the DUT.</p>
<p>MOS dimensions and multiplicities are unchanged from the fixed IHP binding.
The source 160 pF capacitor becomes forty 51.64 um square MIM units, nominally
160.331872 pF total. The source 2 kohm compensation resistor becomes one
W=1 um / L=1.325 um <code>rhigh</code>; each source 9 kohm divider resistor becomes one
W=1 um / L=6.235 um <code>rhigh</code>, with m=1 and b=0. Source capacitor PLUS is
<code>czero</code>, MINUS is <code>vout</code>; the series resistor connects <code>egate</code> to <code>czero</code>.
Divider upper/lower connect <code>vout–fb</code> and <code>fb–vss</code>. Every passive stays physical.</p>
<p>The external source bias is unchanged at 20 uA from VDD to NBIAS. The external
reference is deliberately changed from the source 0.6 V to <strong>0.5 V</strong>, giving
a nominal 1.0 V output and defined headroom at 1.2/1.3 V supply. There is no
separate internal or external COUT; compensation still provides physical
output-connected capacitance. This is not a claim of zero internal storage.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<ul>
<li><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code>: authoritative native LVS circuit.</li>
<li><code>materials/circuit.spice</code>: equivalent simulator representation.</li>
<li><code>materials/testbench.spice</code>: DC bias, bilateral loop measurements and load steps.</li>
<li><code>materials/startup.spice</code>: zero-state resistive-load startup.</li>
<li><code>materials/sweeps.spice</code>: DC line/load and regulation-headroom sweeps.</li>
<li><code>materials/fast.spice</code>: short, finely resolved load-impulse response.</li>
</ul>
<p>Ordered ports: <code>vdd vout vss nbias vref fb sense</code>. VDD/VSS are supply/return;
VOUT is output; NBIAS receives the supply-fed current; VREF receives the 0.5 V
reference. FB is the physical divider junction and SENSE is the amplifier's
feedback-input gate. Connect SENSE externally to FB through the zero-volt
measurement source. This external link replaces the raw VLP's measurement role;
it is not a manufactured voltage source. The relocated break exposes the
actual input/divider boundary; the upper divider still connects to output.
PMOS bodies connect through the VDD well tap; NMOS/poly bodies through the VSS
tap. Preserve every W/L/m, body connection and physical passive. Equivalent
parallel finger arrangements require native LVS and every other check to pass.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Use typical IHP LV MOS, MIM and resistor models at 27 C. Main conditions are
all combinations of VDD=1.2/1.3 V and initial load 0.1/0.5 mA. The current
load doubles at 2–2.02 us and returns at 10.02–10.04 us. Run to 18 us with
1 ns maximum step, Gear order 2, <code>rshunt=1e12</code>, <code>reltol=1e-5</code>, <code>abstol=1e-14</code>
and <code>vntol=1e-8</code>. Startup and fast tests use the same accuracy tolerances.</p>
<p>For AC, independent supply/reference/load sources have no AC excitation.
Perform two sweeps, 0.01 Hz–1 GHz at 300 points/decade, at the same operating
point. VPROBE is oriented SENSE→FB and IINJ from ground→SENSE. Both have
zero DC value and are ordinary external test sources. First set VPROBE AC=1 V,
IINJ AC=0; second set VPROBE AC=0, IINJ AC=1 A. The 1 A is only a normalized
linear AC excitation, not a physical large-signal load.</p>
<p>Let B=−I(VPROBE)/1 V and D=V(SENSE)/1 V from the first sweep; A=−I(VPROBE)/1 A
and C=V(SENSE)/1 A from the second. With delta=AD−BC, define
<code>T=(2*delta−A+D)/(1−2*delta+A−D)</code>. This bilateral two-port return ratio includes
both directional transmissions and port loading. The old voltage-only
<code>−V(FB)/V(SENSE)</code> is not the accepted loop metric. Reset the circuit before
transient analysis; neither AC experiment changes its DC or transient topology.</p>
<p>This measures the declared external feedback loop with the internal circuit
retained. It is not an exhaustive pole proof for all internal device loops or
an RF/model-validity claim beyond the declared tests. Both first and final crossing phase margins must lie in [0, 180] degrees.
The full-sweep return-distance and return-phase metrics have only nonnegative
domain bounds; high-frequency gain is unbounded. They affect continuous
quality without adding a stability acceptance screen.</p>
<p>Startup uses 1/10 kohm loads and simultaneous linear supply/reference/bias
ramps from zero over 1/10 us: eight combinations including both supplies.
Run with <code>uic</code>, no internal-node initial conditions, to 30 us with 2 ns
maximum step. Startup qualification is resistive-load and synchronized-ramp
only, not arbitrary reference sequencing or constant-current startup.</p>
<p>DC sweeps: supply decreases from 1.3 to 0.8 V by 1 mV at 0.1/1 mA; line
span is measured over 1.2–1.3 V. Regulation floor is the first increasing
crossing of <code>abs(VOUT−1.0)=0.03 V</code>. Headroom is supply <strong>minus actual output at
that crossing</strong>, not supply minus the nominal target. The lower supply sweep
is a boundary probe, not an extended qualified operating range. Separately
sweep load 0.1–1 mA by 10 uA at each qualified supply, retaining both endpoints.</p>
<p>Fast conditions use the four main DC biases and a +1 uA load pulse beginning
at 1 ns, 5 ps rise/fall and 1 ns high time. Run 100 ns with 5 ps maximum step.
It probes fast recovery around the solved operating point without modifying
compensation, adding an output capacitor or clamping an internal node.</p>
<p>Transient extrema and AC sweep screens use the saved in-window samples;
DC crossing locations and mean power use interpolation/integration. Numerical
qualification also checks interpolated recovery-window endpoints.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Submit GDSII top cell <code>ldo_008_fer_mirror_ota</code>, at most 10 MiB. Native main/maximal
DRC has no waivers (standalone scope, density/antenna disabled). Strict named-port
LVS includes physical taps. Functional outline is at most 3350 × 310 um and
includes all device, passive, contact and routing drawing layers; annotation
and pin-purpose layers do not contribute. Magic candidate RC must preserve
all physical MOS/MIM/poly and interconnect parasitics. Half-grid import is
explicit; extraction idealizes taps whereas source simulation uses their finite
models. Distributed substrate resistance, PVT, mismatch, noise, fabrication
signoff and arbitrary-load stability are outside qualification.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each <code>source_*</code> job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.</p>
<p>For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.</li>
<li>Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.</li>
<li>Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Scoring uses <code>layout</code>. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.</p>
<p>Measurement definitions below use the supplied SPICE node/source names.
<code>v(n)</code> is node voltage and <code>i(V)</code> is current into a voltage source; delivered
power therefore uses a minus sign. <code>db(z)=20*log10(abs(z))</code>, <code>mag/abs</code>
denote magnitude, and <code>cph</code> is continuous phase in radians. <code>find</code>, <code>when</code>,
<code>from/to</code>, and <code>rise/fall</code> retain the deck's interpolation, window and
crossing conventions; SPICE suffixes p/n/u/m/meg/g mean pico/nano/micro/
milli/mega/giga. Nested <code>avg</code>, <code>min</code>, <code>max</code>, <code>vecmin/vecmax</code> and <code>deriv</code>
mean the stated window average, extrema and sampled derivative. All
declared conditions are measured separately and paired with the same
source condition; a group uses its worst paired quality.</p>

12:T2ecf,<p>Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each <code>source_*</code> job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.</p>
<p>For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.</li>
<li>Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.</li>
<li>Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Scoring uses <code>layout</code>. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.</p>
<p>Measurement definitions below use the supplied SPICE node/source names.
<code>v(n)</code> is node voltage and <code>i(V)</code> is current into a voltage source; delivered
power therefore uses a minus sign. <code>db(z)=20*log10(abs(z))</code>, <code>mag/abs</code>
denote magnitude, and <code>cph</code> is continuous phase in radians. <code>find</code>, <code>when</code>,
<code>from/to</code>, and <code>rise/fall</code> retain the deck's interpolation, window and
crossing conventions; SPICE suffixes p/n/u/m/meg/g mean pico/nano/micro/
milli/mega/giga. Nested <code>avg</code>, <code>min</code>, <code>max</code>, <code>vecmin/vecmax</code> and <code>deriv</code>
mean the stated window average, extrema and sampled derivative. All
declared conditions are measured separately and paired with the same
source condition; a group uses its worst paired quality.</p>
