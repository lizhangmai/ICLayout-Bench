<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>ldo_007_pmos</code> as a GDS layout preserving the complete declared circuit. Five-transistor error amplifier, NMOS bias diode and PMOS pass array with complete resistive feedback divider, feedforward CFF across the upper divider, and series RZ/CC across the pass stage. Seven bound MOS expand to 146 units. The 0 V VLP feedback marker becomes a wire; ideal reference and current bias remain external apparatus.</p>
<h3 id="problem-optimization-objective">Optimization objective</h3>
<p>Maximize the declared quality score within the solve budget while satisfying all physical and necessary functional requirements. The first passing DRC, LVS and required electrical checks establish a feasible baseline. Continue improving the scored metrics from that baseline; a passing check or <code>task_success = true</code> does not mean that the quality objectives have been achieved.</p>

<ul>

<li><code>materials/circuit.spice</code>: equivalent source simulation including physical passives.</li>

<p>Ordered ports: <code>vdd vout vss ebias vref</code>. <code>vss</code> is ground, <code>vdd</code> the positive rail; signal/output and bias/reference ports have the functions and directions specified below. Every named interface must be preserved. Device multipliers are explicitly expanded; series/parallel passive realizations are already declared in the circuit. The reference layout and development source are not solver inputs.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Nominal supply 1.8 V, external reference 1.05 V and 60 uA from VDD into ebias; 27 C, typical GF180 3.3 V models with statistics disabled. Rtop=5 kohm and Rbottom=35 kohm set nominal output 1.2 V. The 140-fold pass array retains 20 um width and 0.5 um length per unit. CFF=60 pF, CC=0.05 pF and RZ=1 ohm are retained. RZ uses twelve parallel native high-poly units (L=1 um, W=97.21 um), nominal 1.000096 ohm at 27 C, to avoid an impossible sub-grid resistor length. No compensation is deleted. External reference-generation and current-source implementation losses are excluded; the 60 uA drawn from VDD and physical divider are included in rail power. External output capacitance is 1 nF with 0.1 ohm series ESR. Evaluate no disturbance, 1-to-2 mA load pulse, and +0.1 V input pulse, plus independent DC points at nominal+0.1 V/1 mA and nominal/2 mA. Pulses start at 100 us, have 1 us edges and 100 us high width; restoration finishes at 202 us. Observe 0–400 us with 10 ns maximum step; pre-step/high/recovery windows are 80–100/180–200/380–400 us. Supply rejection is measured at 1 kHz from a 1 V AC supply input (100 samples/decade, 1 Hz–100 MHz). Ascending DC sweeps cover nominal supply to nominal+0.1 V in 10 mV increments at 1 mA and 1–2 mA load in 0.1 mA increments at nominal supply; adjustment metrics are absolute endpoint slopes. These are finite ranges, not dropout measurements. Startup ramps VDD from zero to nominal in 10 us with fixed external references and a resistive 1 mA nominal load (target voltage/1 mA); it uses the ordinary initial DC solution, no UIC or forced initial output, and observes through 400 us. Startup extrema and late-window output/ripple are diagnostics. No dropout, loop gain or phase margin is claimed. Source and candidate use identical decks and parameters; no numerical shunt is used.</p>
<p>An additional upstream load-step fixture uses the original 1 uF output capacitor and 1-to-10 mA step at 1.8 V. It starts at 5 us, uses 10 ns edges and a 20 us pulse, and stops at 30 us. It reports undershoot and the last 1 mV final-output error crossing. The previous 1 nF apparatus remains a separately declared stress condition. These upstream observations are paired with pre-layout. Undershoot carries 20% using saturating-ratio quality with a 1 uV scale; recovery time remains diagnostic. No accuracy cutoff is imposed.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Use native GF180MCU D standalone-module FEOL/BEOL, connectivity, grid, dummy and antenna rules, excluding chip density/seal-ring coverage. Pass named-port LVS and extract distributed candidate Magic RC. Functional area includes active/poly, passives and all routing metal/vias. Capacitor-substrate and resistor-terminal models follow the frozen PDK configuration, with no DRC waivers. Core topology, bodies and physical compensation are fixed; geometrically equivalent implementations must preserve named ports and device parameters. Maximum outline is 20000 × 2000 um, limiting supported module area rather than electrical quality.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Artifact, geometry, DRC, LVS and declared functional requirements must pass. Parasitic extraction is also mandatory: it must produce a complete, electrically consistent netlist usable by the required post-layout simulations. A completed candidate rejection scores zero; infrastructure errors do not establish candidate failure.</p>
<p>For <code>layout</code> scoring, cap every positively weighted raw quality at 1 before aggregation; the score ranges from 0 to 100. Reaching every weighted target scores 100, and no minimum score is required for validity.</p>
<p>Physical checks and the declared functional bounds must pass. Quality scoring imposes no fixed degradation allowance. Each <code>source_*</code> job and its corresponding candidate extracted circuit use exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters, loads and measurement windows. Independent source observations remain required evidence. The explicit quality targets below supply scoring anchors; otherwise the paired source value is used. Reports retain every paired result.</p>
<p>Let x be the post-layout measurement and b the declared quality target, or the same-condition source measurement when no target is declared:</p>
<ul>
<li>Ratio normalization: maximize with q = x/b and minimize with q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared, use (x+s)/(b+s) and its reciprocal, respectively. The scale handles zero values as a normalization floor; it is neither an allowed degradation nor an acceptance bound.</li>
<li>Amplitude in dB: maximize with q = 10^((x-b)/20) and minimize with its reciprocal.</li>
<li>Target normalization: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), where s is the declared physical scale. Signed or zero-valued operating points are not divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use <code>layout</code> scoring. First compute each metric's raw quality q from the worst paired condition, with raw area quality q_area = area_target / candidate_functional_area. Credit c_i = min(1, q_i) for every positively weighted metric, then compute S = 100 * product(c_i ** w_i). The weights sum to 1 and the score is bounded by 100. An improvement beyond one target cannot compensate for missing another target. Physical, extraction, or functional rejection scores zero; other missing or invalid measurements leave the score unknown, and zero-weight observations must also be valid. Reaching every positively weighted target scores 100; a feasible reference may score far below 100. Weights express quality tradeoffs, not acceptance bounds.</p>
<p>For metrics declaring <code>saturating_ratio</code>, q = 2(b+s)/(b+x+2*s), using the quality anchor b and the positive scale s in the metric’s units. Equality to the anchor gives raw quality 1; improvements approach raw quality 2 but credited quality stays at 1. Normalize each condition before selecting the worst quality. These scales and targets do not add acceptance cutoffs.</p>
