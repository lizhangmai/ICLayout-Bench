11:T330c,<h1 id="problem-single-capacitor-three-stage-feedforward-ota-layout-task">Single-Capacitor Three-Stage Feedforward OTA Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Lay out <code>amp_003_fan_smc</code> with its physical compensation and fixed transistor network,
for the declared loop response and sustained unity-feedback step recovery.</p>
<p>The folded PMOS-input stage drives <code>net050</code>; the PMOS stage at <code>net043</code>
and its NMOS mirror drive <code>net049</code>, which controls the NMOS output device.
An active feedforward path drives the output PMOS directly from <code>net050</code>.
A single physical capacitor branch connects <code>net050</code> to output. This is the
single-capacitor representative: there is no second <code>net049</code> capacitor or
series nulling resistor. The large retained mirror multiplicities and coupled
three-stage/feedforward paths require their own loaded recovery calibration.</p>
<p>The maintained circuit retains all 24 source MOS groups as 412 physical
fingers, 2 MIM units and 0 high-poly segments. All MOS dimensions/multiplicities are retained exactly.
The maintained 3 uA sink is external through <code>net013</code>.
The source value was 60 uA; the maintained 3 uA bias is a deliberate headroom calibration, not an unchanged upstream operating point. Physical
substrate and separate N-well contacts retain the input PMOS bodies at their
common source <code>net31</code>, electrically separate from the VDD well. No internal
ideal bias source or output servo is introduced. Capacitor values are rounded
to physical geometry; every branch remains internal to the DUT.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<ul>
<li><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code>: authoritative native LVS circuit.</li>
<li><code>materials/circuit.spice</code>: matching process-model circuit.</li>
<li><code>materials/testbench.spice</code>: public bias, AC injection and transient deck.</li>
</ul>
<p>Ordered ports: <code>vss vdd vinn vinp vout net013</code>. VSS is return, VDD is supply,
VINN/VINP are inverting/noninverting inputs, VOUT is output, and <code>net013</code>
connects the external 3 uA sink to VSS. Input PMOS bodies retain
the separate source-tied well; other PMOS bodies use the VDD well and NMOS
bodies use the VSS substrate contact. Preserve device W/L/m, body connectivity,
all internal branches and total multiplicities in the authoritative netlist.
Native equivalent parallel fingers are allowed only when LVS and electrical
requirements pass. Do not short distinct ports or create extra driven monitors.</p>
<p>Internal passives, distinct from the external load:</p>
<ul>
<li><code>c0</code>: <code>net050</code> → <code>vout</code>, 2 parallel <code>cap_cmim</code> unit(s), each 40.825 × 40.825 um; nominal total 5.0131059 pF.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Use typical MOS/MIM/poly models, 27 C, VDD=1.2 V and the stated external bias.
All output loads 5/10/20 pF must pass. At DC, VINP=0.5 V and VOUT drives VINN
through a zero-volt measurement source. AC injects 1 V in series with this
feedback connection, with VINP AC=0. The return ratio is
<code>T=-V(vout)/V(vm)</code> at this high-impedance MOS input boundary. Sweep 0.01 Hz
to 100 MHz at 150 points/decade. Use continuous phase without an arbitrary
low-frequency phase-offset correction; the first descending 0 dB crossing
defines unity frequency and <code>180+phase(T)</code> there defines phase margin.
A missing crossing is an evaluator error.</p>
<p>VINP rises 0.5→0.7 V at 2–2.02 us and falls at 10.02–10.04 us, repeating
every 16 us. Simulate 0–18 us with Gear order 2, 2 ns maximum step,
<code>rshunt=1e12</code>, <code>reltol=1e-5</code>, <code>abstol=1e-14</code>, <code>vntol=1e-8</code>.
The numerical shunt is not a manufactured bias or startup device.
PVT, mismatch, noise, distortion, rail-to-rail operation, supply startup and
arbitrary-load stability remain outside the contract.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Submit GDSII top cell <code>amp_003_fan_smc</code>, at most 10 MiB. Pass native IHP main/maximal
DRC without waivers (standalone scope; density/antenna disabled), strict
named-interface LVS with explicit taps and a 2800 × 130 um
functional envelope. All functional device, contact and routing drawing layers
listed in the runtime <code>outline</code> constraint contribute to bounding-box area;
annotation and pin-purpose layers do not.</p>
<p>Post-layout simulation consumes candidate-derived Magic RC with the physical
MIM/poly devices and interconnect parasitics. Half-grid GDS import is explicit.
Magic idealizes substrate/well taps; source simulation retains finite tap models.
Distributed substrate resistance/noise and fabrication signoff are unqualified.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each <code>source_*</code> job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.</p>
<p>For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.</li>
<li>Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.</li>
<li>Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Scoring uses <code>layout</code>. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.</p>
<p>Measurement definitions below use the supplied SPICE node/source names.
<code>v(n)</code> is node voltage and <code>i(V)</code> is current into a voltage source; delivered
power therefore uses a minus sign. <code>db(z)=20*log10(abs(z))</code>, <code>mag/abs</code>
denote magnitude, and <code>cph</code> is continuous phase in radians. <code>find</code>, <code>when</code>,
<code>from/to</code>, and <code>rise/fall</code> retain the deck's interpolation, window and
crossing conventions; SPICE suffixes p/n/u/m/meg/g mean pico/nano/micro/
milli/mega/giga. Nested <code>avg</code>, <code>min</code>, <code>max</code>, <code>vecmin/vecmax</code> and <code>deriv</code>
mean the stated window average, extrema and sampled derivative. All
declared conditions are measured separately and paired with the same
source condition; a group uses its worst paired quality.</p>
