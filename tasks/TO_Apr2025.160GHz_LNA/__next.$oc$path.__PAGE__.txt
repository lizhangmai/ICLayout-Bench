4:T2672,<h1 id="problem-160-ghz-sige-low-noise-amplifier">160 GHz SiGe Low-Noise Amplifier</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement the supplied four-stage common-emitter LNA in IHP SG13G2. Preserve the complete circuit and supplied fixed RF passives. Optimize worst in-band gain and input/output matching against the paired source simulation. A physically valid reference establishes feasibility; it need not meet a performance target.</p>
<h3 id="problem-optimization-objective">Optimization objective</h3>
<p>Maximize the declared quality score within the solve budget while satisfying all physical and necessary functional requirements. The first passing DRC, LVS and required electrical checks establish a feasible baseline. Continue improving the scored metrics from that baseline; a passing check or <code>task_success = true</code> does not mean that the quality objectives have been achieved.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code> is the complete physical core with four HBTs, eight resistors, three MIM decoupler banks and nine three-terminal RF MIM capacitors. Preserve every model, dimension, multiplier, connection and ordered top-level terminal. <code>materials/circuit.spice</code> is its declared simulation representation. Native <code>rfcmim</code> is an explicit parameter-preserving alias of the PDK <code>cap_rfcmim</code>, including each capacitor's actual feed width.</p>
<p><code>materials/rf-macros.gds</code>, <code>rf-macros.json</code> and <code>rf-models.spice</code> supply twelve fixed transmission-line cells, the BEOL ground context and the pinned upstream S2P conversions. The public amplifier ports are RF_INPUT, RF_OUTPUT, VCC, VBB1, VBB2 and GND; the core additionally exposes four choke, four emitter-degeneration and four shunt matching terminals named in its subcircuit declaration. Connect only at the declared physical landings.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Use typical HBT/resistor/capacitor models at 26.85 °C, VCC=1.35 V, VBB1=0.90 V and VBB2=0.93 V. Both ports have a 50 Ω reference and 5 µF fixture DC blocks. The paired source and candidate use the same 109-point linear sweep from 146 to 173 GHz, inside the supplied model coverage. The original independent sources with equal voltage are grouped onto the physical rails; all physical decouplers remain present. The fixture supplies a 1 TΩ numerical reference shunt.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Submit a single GDS top cell <code>FMD_QNC_04_160GHz_LNA</code>, at most 32 MiB, within a 1200×1200 µm functional outline. Native DRC, strict circuit/port LVS and fixed-macro checks must pass. Only individually enumerated inherited curved-edge and recommended package-exit markers are excepted; core spacing, enclosure and functional rules remain enforced. Exceptions retain their declared cell and coordinate scope, so a relocated assembly must still satisfy the complete DRC plan.</p>
<p>Place exactly one instance of every supplied protected cell, preserving its per-layer geometry and the assembly's relative pose. A common grid-aligned translation, 90-degree rotation or mirror is allowed. Make free-core connections at declared landings; do not modify, duplicate, flatten-copy or bypass fixed RF bodies. Matching lines are shunt networks, not series replacements.</p>
<p>The evaluator verifies macro geometry, then replaces each RF body with its existing terminal landings for native extraction. It retains all native devices and extracts nominal 2.5D coupling capacitance from candidate geometry using KPEX. Fixed RF line response comes from the upstream S2P models. This co-simulation includes candidate capacitance; it does not model free-wire R/L or new full-wave interactions. Physical boundaries were reconstructed from upstream GDS and native terminals; independent upstream EM port-plane calibration is unavailable. The task does not claim that additional accuracy.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Finite completed operating-point and RF measurements are required. Squared reflections and peak-to-peak delay must be nonnegative; no quality-score floor or reference-relative performance cutoff determines feasibility.</p>
<p>For each metric, degradation is measured against the frozen source under the same conditions. Quality is 1 for equal or better performance and <code>1/(1+d/s)</code> for positive degradation <code>d</code> at scale <code>s</code>. The final score is 100 times the weighted geometric mean, after physical and execution validity checks.</p>
