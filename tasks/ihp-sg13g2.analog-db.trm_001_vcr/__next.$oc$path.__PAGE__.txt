4:T3069,<h1 id="problem-resistor-pullup-nmos-shunt-trim-layout-task">Resistor-Pullup NMOS Shunt Trim Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>trm_001_vcr</code>, a voltage-controlled NMOS shunt with a physical
approximately 200 kohm supply pullup. Preserve the source W=2 um / L=0.5 um
NMOS, eight series high-poly segments and explicit substrate tap. Qualification
measures loaded trim range, monotonic control, operating-point conductance,
temperature/load sensitivity and finite control-step recovery. This is an
unbuffered nonlinear control node, not an ideal programmable resistor or DAC.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<ul>
<li><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code>: authoritative native LVS circuit.</li>
<li><code>materials/circuit.spice</code>: matching process-model representation.</li>
<li><code>materials/testbench.spice</code>: control sweeps, conductance and loaded recovery.</li>
</ul>
<p>Ordered ports: <code>vdd vcode vout vss</code>. VDD is supply, VCODE controls the shunt
MOS gate, VOUT is the pullup/shunt junction and VSS is return. The NMOS source
and substrate connect through the explicit VSS tap; the physical poly body
uses the same substrate. Eight series <code>rhigh</code> segments connect VDD to VOUT,
each W=1 um / L=17.465 um, m=1 and b=0. They replace the source ideal 200 kohm
resistor and remain inside the DUT. Preserve device models, dimensions and
connectivity; native equivalent resistor merging is allowed only when LVS and
all other checks pass. There are no internal ideal sources or extra bias ports.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Typical IHP LV MOS/poly models, all eight combinations of supplies 1.1/1.3 V,
temperatures 27/85 C and external output loads 500 kohm/1 Mohm. A 1 pF output
load is external test apparatus. DC operating point uses VCODE=0.4 V.
Sweep VCODE from 0.2 to 0.8 V in 2 mV increments; every sampled slope must
satisfy maximum_slope &lt;= 0 V/V: increasing NMOS gate control must not
increase the pullup/shunt output voltage. This is the declared sampled sweep,
not a proof of global monotonicity beyond the control interval.</p>
<p>For transient, VCODE rises 0.3→0.5 V at 2–2.02 us and falls at
10.02–10.04 us, repeating every 16 us. Run to 18 us with 2 ns maximum step,
Gear order 2, <code>rshunt=1e12</code>, <code>reltol=1e-5</code>, <code>abstol=1e-14</code>, <code>vntol=1e-8</code>.
No output clamp, feedback servo or ideal storage is internal to the DUT.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Submit GDSII top cell <code>trm_001_vcr</code>, at most 10 MiB. Pass native main/maximal DRC
without waivers (standalone scope, density/antenna disabled), strict named-port
LVS with explicit tap and a 110 × 80 um functional outline. The runtime
outline includes complete device, resistor, contact and routing drawing layers;
annotation/pin-purpose layers do not contribute to its area.
Candidate Magic RC must preserve physical MOS/poly and interconnect parasitics.
The extractor idealizes the substrate contact; source simulation includes the
finite tap model. Distributed substrate effects, PVT, mismatch, noise,
precision trimming and fabrication signoff remain outside qualification.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each <code>source_*</code> job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.</p>
<p>For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.</li>
<li>Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.</li>
<li>Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Scoring uses <code>layout</code>. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.</p>
<p>Measurement definitions below use the supplied SPICE node/source names.
<code>v(n)</code> is node voltage and <code>i(V)</code> is current into a voltage source; delivered
power therefore uses a minus sign. <code>db(z)=20*log10(abs(z))</code>, <code>mag/abs</code>
denote magnitude, and <code>cph</code> is continuous phase in radians. <code>find</code>, <code>when</code>,
<code>from/to</code>, and <code>rise/fall</code> retain the deck's interpolation, window and
crossing conventions; SPICE suffixes p/n/u/m/meg/g mean pico/nano/micro/
milli/mega/giga. Nested <code>avg</code>, <code>min</code>, <code>max</code>, <code>vecmin/vecmax</code> and <code>deriv</code>
mean the stated window average, extrema and sampled derivative. All
declared conditions are measured separately and paired with the same
source condition; a group uses its worst paired quality.</p>
