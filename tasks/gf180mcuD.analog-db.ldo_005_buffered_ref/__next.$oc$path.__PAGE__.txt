4:T392e,<h1 id="problem-buffered-reference-ldo-layout-task">Buffered-Reference LDO Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>ldo_005_buffered_ref</code> as a GF180MCU D layout. The fixed upstream GF180 6 V circuit combines its self-biased reference amplifier, gain-two reference divider, RC low-pass filter, self-biased error amplifier, PMOS pass device, bleeder and on-die output capacitor. The two inlined amplifiers retain this LDO accession's own dimensions and compensation values; they are not replaced by the standalone amplifier cases.</p>
<h3 id="problem-optimization-objective">Optimization objective</h3>
<p>Maximize the declared quality score within the solve budget while satisfying all physical and necessary functional requirements. The first passing DRC, LVS and required electrical checks establish a feasible baseline. Continue improving the scored metrics from that baseline; a passing check or <code>task_success = true</code> does not mean that the quality objectives have been achieved.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code> is the physical authority; <code>materials/circuit.spice</code>
contains matching native simulator wrappers. <code>materials/testbench.spice</code> defines
all measurements. Ordered ports: <code>vdd vout vss vref</code>.</p>
<p><code>vdd/vss</code>: supply and return; <code>vout</code>: regulated output; <code>vref</code>: external 0.8 V reference. The upstream ideal VREF becomes test apparatus at this explicit port. Its zero-volt VLP loop marker becomes a wire to the output; no physical functional element is removed. The reference divider, RC filter, bleeder and four internal capacitors remain inside the DUT. External load and output capacitor remain test apparatus.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Typical native models at 27 C, statistics disabled. All four combinations of 3.0/3.3 V supply and 50/200 uA DC load are evaluated, with a 0.8 V external reference and 1 uF external output load. AC supply injection spans 1 Hz–1 MHz, 80 points/decade. The load doubles at 100 us and returns after 100 us, with 1 us edges; transient evaluation ends at 500 us with 0.1 us requested steps. This is steady-state load-step characterization, not cold-start or dropout qualification.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>GDS database unit 0.001 um, maximum 10 MiB, top cell <code>ldo_005_buffered_ref</code>.
Native standalone DRC covers geometry, connectivity, manufacturing grid, dummy
fill and antenna; chip-level density and seal-ring closure are excluded.
LVS must match all devices, parameters and named ports. Metal1 labels identify
ports. The functional bounding rectangle includes the device, well, implant,
contact, passive and complete routing layers enumerated in the runtime outline;
text annotations are excluded. Maximum outline is 5000 by 1000 um.</p>
<p>6 V MOS minimum channel lengths are 0.70 um NMOS and 0.55 um PMOS. Only subminimum lengths are raised, and dimensions are quantized to 5 nm. Passives use 1 kohm/square poly, with series segmentation, and 2 fF/um2 MIM-B on M4/M5, with parallel tiling. Source and extracted candidates use the same native layer-specific capacitor models. Geometry, parasitic effects and physical values are authoritative; no ideal R/C replacements are made during post-layout simulation.</p>
<p>Candidate-derived native Magic coupling capacitance and distributed wire
resistance feed post-layout simulation, with zero omission thresholds.
Physical MOS diffusion areas/perimeters come from the layout. Substrate/well
connections are ideal: distributed substrate resistance/noise and inductance
are not represented. Nominal DC regulation, 1 kHz supply rejection and load-step excursion/recovery across four supply/load points. Coefficient 7 reflects interacting regulation stages and recovery across loads. Noise, loop return-ratio, cold start, dropout, PVT and statistical yield are outside this declared scope.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Artifact, geometry, DRC, LVS and declared functional requirements must pass. Parasitic extraction is also mandatory: it must produce a complete, electrically consistent netlist usable by the required post-layout simulations. A completed candidate rejection scores zero; infrastructure errors do not establish candidate failure.</p>
<p>Physical checks and the declared functional bounds must pass. Quality scoring imposes no fixed degradation allowance. Each <code>source_*</code> job and its corresponding candidate extracted circuit use exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters, loads and measurement windows. Independent source observations define the 100-point electrical baseline without depending on the submitted GDS; reports retain every paired result.</p>
<p>Let x be the post-layout measurement and b the source-circuit measurement under the same conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>Ratio normalization: maximize with q = x/b and minimize with q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared, use (x+s)/(b+s) and its reciprocal, respectively. The scale handles zero values as a normalization floor; it is neither an allowed degradation nor an acceptance bound.</li>
<li>Amplitude in dB: maximize with q = 10^((x-b)/20) and minimize with its reciprocal.</li>
<li>Target normalization: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), where s is the declared physical scale. Signed or zero-valued operating points are not divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use <code>layout</code> scoring. Each metric takes its worst paired quality q, and the total score is S = 100 * product(min(1, q_i) ** w_i), with area quality q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to 1; metric dimensions describe measurements and do not determine weights. Physical, extraction, or functional rejection scores zero; other missing or invalid measurements leave the score unknown, and zero-weight observations must also be valid. Matching source-circuit performance at the reference area scores 100 points; each metric is capped at quality 1, so scores cannot exceed 100. Weights express quality tradeoffs rather than hard bounds.</p>
<p><code>regulation_error_v</code>, <code>excursion_v</code> use <code>saturating_ratio</code>: q = 2(b+s)/(b+x+2s), where x is candidate error, b is paired source error and s is the positive scale below. Source equivalence gives q=1; improvement approaches the upper limit 2 with diminishing returns. The scale retains its declared unit and numerical-floor meaning. Normalize each condition before taking the worst quality; other rules follow their declarations.</p>

Repeat as needed and use batch or headless operation. Local manuals, command references, and release notes may be available within the declared EDA installation and PDK resource directories. Locate these resources through <code>/protocol/task.json</code> and consult the documentation for the installed tool version when diagnosing unfamiliar syntax or tool failures.</li>
<li>Generate an initial candidate and submit a snapshot early. When <code>process-feedback</code> is available, check the current GDS with layout MCP <code>check</code> or <code>python -I /protocol/process_check.py</code> before the final submission. Acceptance confirms delivery; the framework independently evaluates validity and quality after the session.</li>
