11:T2476,<h1 id="problem-buffered-reference-ldo-layout-task">Buffered-Reference LDO Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>ldo_005_buffered_ref</code> as a GF180MCU D layout. The fixed upstream GF180 6 V circuit combines its self-biased reference amplifier, gain-two reference divider, RC low-pass filter, self-biased error amplifier, PMOS pass device, bleeder and on-die output capacitor. The two inlined amplifiers retain this LDO accession's own dimensions and compensation values; they are not replaced by the standalone amplifier cases.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code> is the physical authority; <code>materials/circuit.spice</code>
contains matching native simulator wrappers. <code>materials/testbench.spice</code> defines
all measurements. Ordered ports: <code>vdd vout vss vref</code>.</p>
<p><code>vdd/vss</code>: supply and return; <code>vout</code>: regulated output; <code>vref</code>: external 0.8 V reference. The upstream ideal VREF becomes test apparatus at this explicit port. Its zero-volt VLP loop marker becomes a wire to the output; no physical functional element is removed. The reference divider, RC filter, bleeder and four internal capacitors remain inside the DUT. External load and output capacitor remain test apparatus.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Typical native models at 27 C, statistics disabled. All four combinations of 3.0/3.3 V supply and 50/200 uA DC load are evaluated, with a 0.8 V external reference and 1 uF external output load. AC supply injection spans 1 Hz–1 MHz, 80 points/decade. The load doubles at 100 us and returns after 100 us, with 1 us edges; transient evaluation ends at 500 us with 0.1 us requested steps. This is steady-state load-step characterization, not cold-start or dropout qualification.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>GDS database unit 0.001 um, maximum 10 MiB, top cell <code>ldo_005_buffered_ref</code>.
Native standalone DRC covers geometry, connectivity, manufacturing grid, dummy
fill and antenna; chip-level density and seal-ring closure are excluded.
LVS must match all devices, parameters and named ports. Metal1 labels identify
ports. The functional bounding rectangle includes the device, well, implant,
contact, passive and complete routing layers enumerated in the runtime outline;
text annotations are excluded. Maximum outline is 5000 by 1000 um.</p>
<p>6 V MOS minimum channel lengths are 0.70 um NMOS and 0.55 um PMOS. Only subminimum lengths are raised, and dimensions are quantized to 5 nm. Passives use 1 kohm/square poly, with series segmentation, and 2 fF/um2 MIM-B on M4/M5, with parallel tiling. Source and extracted candidates use the same native layer-specific capacitor models. Geometry, parasitic effects and physical values are authoritative; no ideal R/C replacements are made during post-layout simulation.</p>
<p>Candidate-derived native Magic coupling capacitance and distributed wire
resistance feed post-layout simulation, with zero omission thresholds.
Physical MOS diffusion areas/perimeters come from the layout. Substrate/well
connections are ideal: distributed substrate resistance/noise and inductance
are not represented. Nominal DC regulation, 1 kHz supply rejection and load-step excursion/recovery across four supply/load points. Coefficient 7 reflects interacting regulation stages and recovery across loads. Noise, loop return-ratio, cold start, dropout, PVT and statistical yield are outside this declared scope.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each <code>source_*</code> job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.</p>
<p>For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.</li>
<li>Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.</li>
<li>Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Scoring uses <code>layout</code>. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.</p>
