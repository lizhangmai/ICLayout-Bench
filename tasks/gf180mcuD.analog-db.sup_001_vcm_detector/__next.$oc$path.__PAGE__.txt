4:T3668,<h1 id="problem-passive-resistive-common-mode-averager-layout-task">Passive Resistive Common-Mode Averager Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Two matched 1 Mohm resistors join vinp to vcm_out and vcm_out to vinn. This is a passive average detector, without a buffer, amplifier or CMFB controller. The fixed IHP binding contains ideal R cards, not process-specific resistor models; the maintained physical adaptation uses forty native GF180 ppolyf_u_1k segments (twenty per branch), totaling 999951.8 ohm per branch at TT/27 C. The extra vss port grounds their physical substrate; it is not a functional supply. Target top cell: <code>sup_001_vcm_detector</code>.</p>
<h3 id="problem-optimization-objective">Optimization objective</h3>
<p>Maximize the declared quality score within the solve budget while satisfying all physical and necessary functional requirements. The first passing DRC, LVS and required electrical checks establish a feasible baseline. Continue improving the scored metrics from that baseline; a passing check or <code>task_success = true</code> does not mean that the quality objectives have been achieved.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code> is the authoritative physical netlist; <code>materials/circuit.spice</code> is the same compact-device circuit for simulation. <code>materials/testbench.spice</code> supplies the external fixture. Both source and extracted candidate use the exact same deck, bias, loads, excitation, initial-state procedure and measurement windows. Ordered ports: vcm_out (sense output), vinp (positive input), vinn (negative input), vss (physical substrate).</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>27 C, TT. External ideal drivers are 0.8 V and 0.4 V, each followed by 1 kohm source resistance. The output has 1 pF and either 1 Mohm or 1 Gohm to ground. Each load uses common-mode AC (both drivers +1 V) and differential AC (+1/-1 V), 1 Hz–10 MHz at 40 points/decade. Both drivers increase by 0.1 V at 1 us, with 10 ns edges, and stay high through the 5 us observation. Maximum transient step is 2 ns. The initial state is the same biased DC operating point for source and candidate. A 1 Gohm load is a finite high-impedance observation, not an unloaded claim. The analytic DC limit is Vcm*RL/(RL+500.5 kohm), tending to Vcm only as RL tends to infinity. Both input-source powers are included; no zero-power claim follows from the absence of a supply pin.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Use native GF180 KLayout main rules, named-interface LVS and reviewed Magic RC extraction on a ten-division grid, without per-task waivers. Extraction must retain all models, multiplicities, dimensions, resistance values, connections and ordered ports. The functional outline includes task-declared devices, wells, contacts and all routing layers; annotations and pin labels do not add area. Maximum outline is 5000 × 1000 um and maximum GDS size is 10485760 bytes. Physically implement all devices and resistors without adding ideal internal macros or auxiliary servos; external sources, probe loads and storage capacitors belong to the testbench.</p>
