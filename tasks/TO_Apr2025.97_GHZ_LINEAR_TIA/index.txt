11:T2b14,<h1 id="problem-97-ghz-sige-linear-tia-layout">97 GHz SiGe linear TIA layout</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement the full four-stage linear TIA, its two complete bias clamps, and all eight fixed upstream RF passives. The submitted top cell is <code>FMD_QNC_01_LIN_TIA</code>. Preserve the declared device geometry and circuit connectivity while improving the RF score through your physical implementation of the free circuit.</p>
<h3 id="problem-optimization-objective">Optimization objective</h3>
<p>Maximize the declared quality score within the solve budget while satisfying all physical and necessary functional requirements. The first passing DRC, LVS and required electrical checks establish a feasible baseline. Continue improving the scored metrics from that baseline; a passing check or <code>task_success = true</code> does not mean that the quality objectives have been achieved.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.cdl</code> defines the complete free core: 12 HBTs, 15 resistors and five MIM capacitor banks. <code>materials/circuit.spice</code> supplies equivalent compact-model calls. <code>materials/rf-models.spice</code> preserves all eight native upstream S2P conversions and the full <code>RF_DUT RFIN RFOUT VCC1 VCC2 GND</code> wrapper. The fixture is <code>materials/testbench.spice</code>.</p>
<p><code>materials/rf-macros.gds</code> and <code>materials/rf-macros.json</code> define the fixed passive cells, their positions, exact physical landings and label layers. Instantiate each of <code>RF_INPUT</code>, <code>RF_OUTPUT</code>, <code>RF_CHOKE1</code> through <code>RF_CHOKE4</code>, <code>RF_DEG1</code>, <code>RF_DEG4</code> and <code>RF_GROUND</code> once. Their complete assembly is available as <code>RF_PASSIVES</code>.</p>
<p>The free core's ordered ports are <code>VCC1 VCC2 GND RF_INPUT_INNER RF_OUTPUT_INNER RF_CHOKE1_INNER RF_CHOKE2_INNER RF_CHOKE3_INNER RF_CHOKE4_INNER RF_DEG1_INNER RF_DEG4_INNER</code>. Connect these to the corresponding macro landings. <code>VCC1</code> and <code>VCC2</code> are distinct power rails; <code>GND</code> includes the HBT substrate return. The two-transistor bias clamps retain distinct output and auxiliary collector nodes; similar names never authorize shorting different CDL nets.</p>
<p>The ordinary npn13G2 emitter has fixed 70 × 900 nm dimensions. The pinned native extractor writes emitter bounding-box X/Y dimensions as <code>we</code>/<code>le</code>, so rotated devices have 900/70 nm annotations. Preserve the declared ordered physical annotations and <code>Nx</code>; simulator calls use the original fixed-geometry <code>Nx</code> interface. The three supply-decoupling banks follow the upstream physical CDL: 20 × 100 µm and two 60 × 60 µm banks with <code>m=2</code>. Both bias decouplers are 30 × 30 µm.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Use typical HBT, resistor and capacitor corners at 26.85 °C. Both physical rails are 3.3 V. The upstream's five ideal DC supplies all have this value; loads are bound to their physical VCC1/VCC2 rails, including choke 2 on VCC2. Input/output ports are 50 Ω and DC-blocked with 5 µF. Sweep 871 equally spaced samples from 10 to 97 GHz, within the inherited S2P coverage through 100 GHz. Front- and back-annotation use the identical fixture, declared SPARSE solver and 1 TΩ numerical shunts for floating PEX conductors. No noise or transient behavior is inferred from deterministic S2P tables.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Pass the declared full native DRC, strict named-port LVS and 1100 × 1100 µm functional outline. Only listed exact curved-RF edge and recommended package-exit markers have exceptions; core spacing, enclosure and connectivity remain checked. Exceptions retain their declared cell and coordinate scope, so a relocated assembly must still satisfy the complete DRC plan.</p>
<p>Preserve each macro's flattened per-layer geometry, text and count. Retain their relative positions and ground context; the complete assembly may translate, reflect or turn by multiples of 90 degrees. Connect free wiring only at the specified signal-body/through-via landing regions. Fixture-only input/output pads have no free-circuit attachment. Provide <code>GND</code> on actual return metal using the declared native label layers.</p>
<p>The evaluator verifies the immutable macros, then exposes their existing terminal metal in an extraction copy. Native LVS checks the full free core and every macro terminal. Candidate local coupling capacitance, all native compact devices and the inherited fixed RF models form the back-annotated circuit. Local wire resistance and inductance are omitted; the fixed S2P models retain their characterized RF R/L/C response. Source devices and reference wiring do not supplement candidate extraction.</p>
<p>This is an explicit RF macro co-simulation approximation. Its physical model boundary is reconstructed from the whole-chip GDS, stage polygons, native connectivity and original schematic port order. Independent EM reference-plane calibration is unavailable. The models do not evaluate newly designed passive geometry or full-wave interactions from arbitrary added routing.</p>

<p>All measurements must be finite. Squared reflections and peak-to-peak delay have nonnegative physical domains. There is no minimum RF score for feasibility.</p>

<p>Solve budget: <strong>1.5 hours</strong>.</p>
<p>Use the existing open EDA image and declared SG13G2 resources. Submit <code>output/final.gds</code> with the complete free circuit and supplied passive assembly. Author code and reference layouts are not task inputs.</p>
<h3 id="problem-solve-workflow">Solve workflow</h3>
<ol>
<li>Read the declared circuit and materials, <code>/protocol/task.json</code> for paths, constraints and reviewed resources, and <code>/protocol/harness.json</code> for capabilities and helper commands. Task inputs under <code>/task</code> and resources under <code>/resources</code> are read-only; construct the layout in <code>/workspace</code> with the declared tools. With layout MCP, paths and commands refer to the remote EDA workspace. Create your own implementation with real devices, interconnect and pins, using your own tool loop without delegating to other agents; reference layouts and author code are not solver inputs.</li>
<li>Inspect <code>resources.external</code> before using external tools. Each command starts a fresh shell: when tool modules are declared, source <code>/usr/share/Modules/init/bash</code> and load the selected tool's modules; export its declared <code>tool_environment</code> settings in that same command. Repeat as needed and use batch or headless operation. Local manuals, command references, and release notes may be available within the declared EDA installation and PDK resource directories. Locate these resources through <code>/protocol/task.json</code> and consult the documentation for the installed tool version when diagnosing unfamiliar syntax or tool failures.</li>
<li>Generate an initial candidate and submit a snapshot early. When <code>process-feedback</code> is available, check the current GDS with layout MCP <code>check</code> or <code>python -I /protocol/process_check.py</code> before the final submission. This runs the final evaluator's complete plan and returns engineering diagnostics and candidate measurements, without scores or normalized quality factors. Checks have no count limit and consume the same wall-clock budget. Inspect the physical and functional results and all scored metrics; successful tool execution alone does not establish requirement satisfaction. Read paginated diagnostics with <code>report</code> when using MCP. Local exploratory checks do not replace this complete check.</li>
<li>Use the remaining time for measured improvements to the declared quality objectives. Identify the limiting scored metrics, make targeted layout changes, regenerate and check the candidate, and compare measurements with the previous candidate and the stated scoring targets and trade-offs. Preserve the best valid candidate and revert changes that worsen the overall objective. Repeat while time and useful improvements remain; passing validity checks alone is not a stopping condition.</li>
<li>Stop optimization when the budget expires, all declared quality objectives are achieved, or measured trials support convergence. If stopping early, explain the changes tried, the metric comparisons and the remaining limitations. Track remaining time through session status. At soft-budget expiry, finish or cancel already-started work and submit immediately; do not start another command, check or optimization round. If an MCP execution is running, use <code>poll</code> with its <code>execution_id</code> and the returned <code>next_offset</code> as <code>offset</code> until completion, or cancel it and wait for it to stop.</li>
<li>Finish or cancel outstanding work, restore your best candidate to the declared output path, and explicitly submit it before ending the session. With layout MCP <code>submit</code>, remove the <code>/workspace/</code> prefix from <code>output.path</code>; with the container interface, run <code>python -I /protocol/submit.py</code>. Wait for the receipt: only the last accepted snapshot is judged, and writing a file alone is not submission. Acceptance confirms delivery; the framework independently evaluates validity and quality after the session.</li>
</ol>
