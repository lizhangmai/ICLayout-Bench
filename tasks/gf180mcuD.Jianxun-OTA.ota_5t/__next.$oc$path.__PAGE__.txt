<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>ota_5t</code> in GF180MCU variant D while preserving the supplied circuit, device dimensions and ordered interface. Small-signal differential gain and unity frequency at 1.65 V common mode, 10 uA bias and 1 pF load.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p>The delivered files are <code>materials/circuit.spice</code> (authoritative circuit), <code>materials/testbench.spice</code> (stimuli and measurements), and this problem. Runtime task metadata supplies the same frozen constraints, evaluation conditions and output declaration.</p>
<p>Ordered subcircuit and GDS interface: <code>vdd, out, in_p, in_n, i_bias, vss</code>.
vdd/vss: supply/return; out: single-ended output; in_p/in_n: differential inputs; i_bias: current-bias input.</p>
<p>Preserve topology and total device width, channel length, multiplicity, resistor dimensions and body connections. Equivalent hierarchy, diffusion sharing and parallel-finger implementations are allowed when they pass connectivity and electrical checks. No source-model replacement, idealized internal device or changed device sizing is permitted. A dummy device remains part of the circuit even when its terminals are tied together.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Use 3.3 V and 27 C, a 10 uA current from vdd into i_bias, input common mode 1.65 V and 1 pF from out to ground. The differential AC excitation is 1 V (in_p: +0.5 V; in_n: -0.5 V). Measure the DC output and supply power, then sweep 10 Hz to 1 GHz at 40 points/decade.</p>
<p>Use the reviewed typical primitive models with statistical variation disabled. Numerical parameters in the runtime simulation jobs supply the testbench's <code>parameters.spice</code>. Follow the delivered testbench for interpolation and detailed PWL definitions. This is a nominal compact-model task; temperature/process corners, mismatch yield, noise, RF/EM and full-chip density closure are outside its qualification scope.</p>

<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Physical checks and declared functional bounds remain mandatory. Quality has no
fixed allowed-degradation threshold. Each <code>source_*</code> job simulates the declared
source circuit with exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters,
load and measurement window as its paired extracted-candidate job. A source
observation is the 100-point electrical baseline; it is independent of the
submitted GDS. All individual pairs are retained in the evaluation report.</p>
<p>For a post-layout observation x and its source observation b:</p>
<ul>
<li>Maximize: q = x/b; minimize: q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared,
use (x+s)/(b+s) or its inverse. This handles zero-valued error measurements;
s is a normalization floor, not an allowed degradation or pass threshold.</li>
<li>Amplitude dB: q = 10^((x-b)/20) for maximize, its inverse for minimize.</li>
<li>Target: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), with a declared physical scale s. Signed and
zero-valued operating points are never divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Scoring uses <code>layout</code>. A metric uses its worst paired quality q. The score is
S = 100 * product(q_i ** w_i), including area quality
q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to
one. Dimensions describe measurements but do not determine their weights.
Physical or functional rejection scores zero; missing or invalid measurements
produce an unknown score, including measurements with zero weight.
Source-equivalent performance at the area reference scores 100; improvements
can exceed 100. A weight expresses a tradeoff, not a hard acceptance limit.</p>
