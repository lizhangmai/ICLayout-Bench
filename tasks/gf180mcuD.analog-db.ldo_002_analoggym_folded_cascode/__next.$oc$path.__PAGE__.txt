4:T45ab,<h1 id="problem-folded-cascode-ldo-layout-task">Folded-Cascode LDO Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>ldo_002_analoggym_folded_cascode</code> as a GDS layout preserving the complete declared circuit. Nine-MOS folded-cascode error amplifier and a PMOS pass array, with direct output feedback, series RZ/CC and the physical minimum-load bleed. The fixed GF180 6 V binding expands to 259 MOS, three 2 fF/um2 MIM units, one nulling resistor and two series bleed segments. VB1, VB2 and VREF remain external sources; the original 0 V VLP measurement marker becomes a wire.</p>
<h3 id="problem-optimization-objective">Optimization objective</h3>
<p>Maximize the declared quality score within the solve budget while satisfying all physical and necessary functional requirements. The first passing DRC, LVS and required electrical checks establish a feasible baseline. Continue improving the scored metrics from that baseline; a passing check or <code>task_success = true</code> does not mean that the quality objectives have been achieved.</p>

<ul>

<li><code>materials/circuit.spice</code>: equivalent source simulation including physical passives.</li>

<p>Ordered ports: <code>vdd vout vss vb1 vb2 vref</code>. <code>vss</code> is ground, <code>vdd</code> the positive rail; signal/output and bias/reference ports have the functions and directions specified below. Every named interface must be preserved. Device multipliers are explicitly expanded; series/parallel passive realizations are already declared in the circuit. The reference layout and development source are not solver inputs.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Nominal supply 2.0 V, external reference 1.8 V, VB1=1.0 V and VB2=0.025 V; 27 C, typical GF180 models with statistical variation disabled. Native 6 V DRC requires NMOS L&gt;=0.70 um and PMOS L&gt;=0.55 um; only undersized lengths are increased to those physical minima. All default widths and the 250-fold 10 um pass-device multiplier are retained. R_bleed is 100 kohm inside the physical DUT and consumes Vout^2/R power; external 1/2 mA load currents are additional, not replacements. CC=2 pF and RZ=3 kohm retain their original connections. External output capacitance is 1 nF with 0.1 ohm series ESR. Evaluate no disturbance, 1-to-2 mA load pulse, and +0.1 V input pulse, plus independent DC points at nominal+0.1 V/1 mA and nominal/2 mA. Pulses start at 100 us, have 1 us edges and 100 us high width; restoration finishes at 202 us. Observe 0–400 us with 10 ns maximum step; pre-step/high/recovery windows are 80–100/180–200/380–400 us. Supply rejection is measured at 1 kHz from a 1 V AC supply input (100 samples/decade, 1 Hz–100 MHz). Ascending DC sweeps cover nominal supply to nominal+0.1 V in 10 mV increments at 1 mA and 1–2 mA load in 0.1 mA increments at nominal supply; adjustment metrics are absolute endpoint slopes. These are finite ranges, not dropout measurements. Startup ramps VDD from zero to nominal in 10 us with fixed external references and a resistive 1 mA nominal load (target voltage/1 mA); it uses the ordinary initial DC solution, no UIC or forced initial output, and observes through 400 us. Startup extrema and late-window output/ripple are diagnostics. No dropout, loop gain or phase margin is claimed. Source and candidate use identical decks and parameters; no numerical shunt is used.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Use native GF180MCU D standalone-module FEOL/BEOL, connectivity, grid, dummy and antenna rules, excluding chip density/seal-ring coverage. Pass named-port LVS and extract distributed candidate Magic RC. Functional area includes active/poly, passives and all routing metal/vias. Capacitor-substrate and resistor-terminal models follow the frozen PDK configuration, with no DRC waivers. Core topology, bodies and physical compensation are fixed; geometrically equivalent implementations must preserve named ports and device parameters. Maximum outline is 20000 × 2000 um, limiting supported module area rather than electrical quality.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Artifact, geometry, DRC, LVS and declared functional requirements must pass. Parasitic extraction is also mandatory: it must produce a complete, electrically consistent netlist usable by the required post-layout simulations. A completed candidate rejection scores zero; infrastructure errors do not establish candidate failure.</p>
<p>Physical checks and the declared functional bounds must pass. Quality scoring imposes no fixed degradation allowance. Each <code>source_*</code> job and its corresponding candidate extracted circuit use exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters, loads and measurement windows. Independent source observations define the 100-point electrical baseline without depending on the submitted GDS; reports retain every paired result.</p>
<p>Let x be the post-layout measurement and b the source-circuit measurement under the same conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>Ratio normalization: maximize with q = x/b and minimize with q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared, use (x+s)/(b+s) and its reciprocal, respectively. The scale handles zero values as a normalization floor; it is neither an allowed degradation nor an acceptance bound.</li>
<li>Amplitude in dB: maximize with q = 10^((x-b)/20) and minimize with its reciprocal.</li>
<li>Target normalization: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), where s is the declared physical scale. Signed or zero-valued operating points are not divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use <code>layout</code> scoring. Each metric takes its worst paired quality q, and the total score is S = 100 * product(min(1, q_i) ** w_i), with area quality q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to 1; metric dimensions describe measurements and do not determine weights. Physical, extraction, or functional rejection scores zero; other missing or invalid measurements leave the score unknown, and zero-weight observations must also be valid. Matching source-circuit performance at the reference area scores 100 points; each metric is capped at quality 1, so scores cannot exceed 100. Weights express quality tradeoffs rather than hard bounds.</p>
<p><code>regulation_error_v</code>, <code>quiet_ripple_v</code>, <code>recovery_ripple_v</code>, <code>late_error_v</code>, <code>excursion_v</code>, <code>line_reg_v_per_v</code>, <code>load_reg_ohm</code> use <code>saturating_ratio</code>: q = 2(b+s)/(b+x+2s), where x is candidate error, b is paired source error and s is the positive scale below. Source-equivalent error gives q=1; improvement approaches the upper limit 2 with diminishing returns. s retains the declared units and numeric-floor meaning. Normalize each condition before taking the worst quality; other normalization and functional rules follow their own declarations.</p>
