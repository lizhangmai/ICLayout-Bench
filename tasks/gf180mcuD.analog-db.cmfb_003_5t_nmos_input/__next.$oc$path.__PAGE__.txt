4:T3bef,<h1 id="problem-segmented-resistor-nmos-common-mode-controller-layout-task">Segmented-Resistor NMOS Common-Mode Controller Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement <code>cmfb_003_5t_nmos_input</code> in gf180mcuD and submit self-contained GDS. Seven MOS devices form an NMOS-input common-mode error amplifier and self-bias network. Each ideal 5 Mohm sense arm is implemented as twenty series GF180 ppolyf_u_1k segments, each 2 um wide and 500 um long: 5,000 squares per arm. The segmentation, substrate terminals and intermediate nodes are part of the maintained circuit. The independent witness uses compact rows connected by metal; it retains both complete resistance chains.</p>
<h3 id="problem-optimization-objective">Optimization objective</h3>
<p>Maximize the declared quality score within the solve budget while satisfying all physical and necessary functional requirements. The first passing DRC, LVS and required electrical checks establish a feasible baseline. Continue improving the scored metrics from that baseline; a passing check or <code>task_success = true</code> does not mean that the quality objectives have been achieved.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.spice</code> is the authoritative physical circuit. The same netlist is used for source calibration. <code>materials/testbench.spice</code> defines measurements, and this problem is the description input.
Ordered ports: <code>vinp vinn vcmfb vref vdd vss</code>. In order: Positive sensed output, negative sensed output, control output, common-mode reference, supply, and return.</p>
<p>Preserve connectivity, W/L/m, passive geometry and body terminals, with physical contacts. Placement and routing are free; splitting and source/drain exchange must satisfy the declared LVS equivalence rules. Statistical matching and common-centroid placement are unscored. Ideal external sources, loads and test apparatus belong to the testbench, outside the DUT.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Temperature 27 C; use typical GF180 3.3 V MOS and high-resistance poly models with statistical variation disabled; no distributed silicon-substrate resistance network is simulated.</p>
<p>VDD = 3.3 V; VSS = 0 V; VREF = 1.65 V. The external first-order inverting plant has drive = 3.3 V - V(vcmfb), followed by 100 kohm and 100 nF to common mode. V(vinp/vinn) = plant output + disturbance +/- d, where d = 0, 0.1 and 0.2 V in separate conditions. Disturbance is 0 through 10 ms, +0.1 V at 10.01 ms through 30 ms, -0.1 V at 30.01 ms through 50 ms, and 0 at 50.01 ms through 70 ms. A 10 pF external capacitor loads vcmfb. Transient output/max step is 10 us. This plant is test apparatus, not an internal ideal servo or a claimed transistor amplifier. Recovery windows are 25–29 ms and 45–49 ms for the positive/negative disturbances; error is measured at the average sensed inputs against VREF. Controller power excludes the external plant.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Top cell <code>cmfb_003_5t_nmos_input</code>, named ports, resolved hierarchy, at most 10 MiB. Pass artifact, GF180 variant-D DRC including antenna, with chip-level density and seal-ring closure outside scope; strict named-port LVS; geometry, without DRC waivers. Functional bounding box must fit within 1100 by 280 um. The footprint includes every process device and routing drawing layer in the frozen runtime outline list: active, wells, implants, poly, contacts, metals/vias and device/passive markers. Annotation and pin-purpose shapes are excluded. All functional routing must use drawing layers.</p>
<p>Every scored simulation consumes the submitted GDS-derived distributed wiring RC and extracted device geometry. Qualification covers recovery with this specified external plant, not stability with arbitrary amplifiers. The long physical sense chains retain candidate-derived parasitics. Startup, mismatch, noise, PVT and a complete differential amplifier are outside scope. Fabrication signoff is not claimed.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Artifact, geometry, DRC, LVS and declared functional requirements must pass. Parasitic extraction is also mandatory: it must produce a complete, electrically consistent netlist usable by the required post-layout simulations. A completed candidate rejection scores zero; infrastructure errors do not establish candidate failure.</p>
<p>All three conditions must complete, and each finite observation must satisfy its inclusive interval; aggregation cannot hide a failed condition. Missing measurements/crossings or incomplete extraction cannot establish success.</p>
<p>Physical checks and the declared functional bounds must pass. Quality scoring imposes no fixed degradation allowance. Each <code>source_*</code> job and its corresponding candidate extracted circuit use exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters, loads and measurement windows. Independent source observations define the 100-point electrical baseline without depending on the submitted GDS; reports retain every paired result.</p>
<p>Let x be the post-layout measurement and b the source-circuit measurement under the same conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>Ratio normalization: maximize with q = x/b and minimize with q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared, use (x+s)/(b+s) and its reciprocal, respectively. The scale handles zero values as a normalization floor; it is neither an allowed degradation nor an acceptance bound.</li>
<li>Amplitude in dB: maximize with q = 10^((x-b)/20) and minimize with its reciprocal.</li>
<li>Target normalization: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), where s is the declared physical scale. Signed or zero-valued operating points are not divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use <code>layout</code> scoring. Each metric takes its worst paired quality q, and the total score is S = 100 * product(min(1, q_i) ** w_i), with area quality q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to 1; metric dimensions describe measurements and do not determine weights. Physical, extraction, or functional rejection scores zero; other missing or invalid measurements leave the score unknown, and zero-weight observations must also be valid. Matching source-circuit performance at the reference area scores 100 points; each metric is capped at quality 1, so scores cannot exceed 100. Weights express quality tradeoffs rather than hard bounds.</p>
<p><code>error_v</code>, <code>peak_error_v</code>, <code>recovery_up_v</code>, <code>recovery_down_v</code>, <code>recovery_zero_v</code> use <code>saturating_ratio</code>: q = 2(b+s)/(b+x+2s), where x is candidate error, b is paired source error and s is the positive scale below. Source equivalence gives q=1; improvement approaches the upper limit 2 with diminishing returns. s retains the declared units and numeric-floor meaning; normalize each condition before taking the worst quality, and apply other rules as declared.</p>
