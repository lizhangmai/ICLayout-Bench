4:T395d,<h1 id="problem-externally-biased-pmos-input-folded-cascode-ota-layout-task">Externally Biased PMOS-Input Folded-Cascode OTA Layout Task</h1>
<h2 id="problem-objective">Objective</h2>
<p>Implement the fixed <code>amp_004_folded_cascode</code> circuit in GF180MCU D and submit self-contained GDS.
Fourteen MOS entries (17 instances after multiplicity expansion) form a PMOS-input folded-cascode OTA with a bias mirror and cascode branches. The two ideal internal voltage sources become explicit vb1/vb2 ports. Total MOS widths, lengths and multiplicities are retained; the maintained devices use single-finger geometry instead of the upstream nf settings. Source and extracted simulation both represent this maintained implementation.</p>
<h3 id="problem-optimization-objective">Optimization objective</h3>
<p>Maximize the declared quality score within the solve budget while satisfying all physical and necessary functional requirements. The first passing DRC, LVS and required electrical checks establish a feasible baseline. Continue improving the scored metrics from that baseline; a passing check or <code>task_success = true</code> does not mean that the quality objectives have been achieved.</p>
<h2 id="problem-inputs-and-interface">Inputs and Interface</h2>
<p><code>materials/circuit.spice</code> is authoritative for LVS and source calibration;
<code>materials/testbench.spice</code> defines every electrical measurement. This problem
is the description input. Ordered ports: <code>vinn vinp vout vdd ibias vss vb1 vb2</code>.
In order, these are: Negative input, positive input, output, supply, PMOS reference-current sink, return, NMOS cascode bias, and PMOS cascode bias.</p>
<p>Preserve netlist connectivity, dimensions, multiplicities and body terminals. Provide physical well/substrate contacts for the declared supplies. Placement, routing, splitting and source/drain exchange must satisfy the declared LVS equivalence rules. Common-centroid placement and statistical matching are unscored. Ideal external stimuli, biases and loads belong to the testbench, outside the DUT.</p>
<h2 id="problem-operating-conditions">Operating Conditions</h2>
<p>Use the typical GF180 3.3 V MOS models and physical poly models where present,
with statistical variation disabled. Unless swept explicitly, temperature is 27 C.
VSS = 0 V; VDD = 2.7, 3.0 and 3.3 V; a 20 uA sink from ibias to VSS; VB1 = 1.2 V and VB2 = 1.95 V; external 1 pF output load. VINP has DC 1.65 V and AC +0.5 V. A 1 TH output-to-VINN inductor closes DC feedback; a 1 F coupling capacitor injects AC -0.5 V at VINN. The open-loop transfer is V(vout)/(V(vinp)-V(vinn)). These large L/C elements are external measurement apparatus. At 2.7 V the reduced headroom lowers gain and shifts the DC operating point.</p>
<p>AC sweeps 100 points per decade from 1 Hz to 1 GHz. Under unit AC injection, gain-stage transfer is V(vout); under unit differential excitation, differential-pair transfer is V(voutn)-V(voutp); OTA transfer is defined above.</p>
<h2 id="problem-physical-requirements">Physical Requirements</h2>
<p>Submit top cell <code>amp_004_folded_cascode</code> with every named electrical port, resolved hierarchy
and a file size at most 10 MiB. Artifact, GF180 variant-D DRC including antenna,
strict named-port LVS and geometry must pass, without DRC waivers. Chip-level
density and seal-ring closure are outside this standalone-block scope.</p>
<p>The functional bounding box must fit within 400 by 340 um. Area includes all
process device and routing drawing layers listed in runtime constraints:
wells, implants, active, poly, contacts, metals/vias and passive/device markers.
Annotation and pin-purpose shapes are excluded; all functional routing must use
drawing layers. The complete layer list is frozen in <code>/protocol/task.json</code>.</p>
<p>The judge independently extracts distributed wiring resistance and capacitance
from the submitted GDS, retaining candidate-derived MOS/passive geometry and
external body connections. Every scored simulation consumes that extracted DUT.
This model boundary does not include a distributed silicon substrate network.
The case measures small-signal unity-crossing phase margin with the declared load. Startup, large-signal settling, other loads, mismatch, noise, PVT and EM are outside scope. Manufacturing signoff is not claimed.</p>
<h2 id="problem-electrical-requirements-and-scoring">Electrical Requirements and Scoring</h2>
<p>Artifact, geometry, DRC, LVS and declared functional requirements must pass. Parasitic extraction is also mandatory: it must produce a complete, electrically consistent netlist usable by the required post-layout simulations. A completed candidate rejection scores zero; infrastructure errors do not establish candidate failure.</p>
<p>Complete all three conditions, with every observation finite and within its inclusive interval. Aggregation must not hide a failed condition; missing crossings or incomplete extraction/simulation cannot establish success. Retain waveforms for independent reconstruction of observations.</p>
<p>Physical checks and the declared functional bounds must pass. Quality scoring imposes no fixed degradation allowance. Each <code>source_*</code> job and its corresponding candidate extracted circuit use exactly the same testbench, model resources, parameters, loads and measurement windows. Independent source observations define the 100-point electrical baseline without depending on the submitted GDS; reports retain every paired result.</p>
<p>Let x be the post-layout measurement and b the source-circuit measurement under the same conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>Ratio normalization: maximize with q = x/b and minimize with q = b/x. When a numerical scale s is declared, use (x+s)/(b+s) and its reciprocal, respectively. The scale handles zero values as a normalization floor; it is neither an allowed degradation nor an acceptance bound.</li>
<li>Amplitude in dB: maximize with q = 10^((x-b)/20) and minimize with its reciprocal.</li>
<li>Target normalization: q = 1/(1+abs(x-b)/s), where s is the declared physical scale. Signed or zero-valued operating points are not divided directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use <code>layout</code> scoring. Each metric takes its worst paired quality q, and the total score is S = 100 * product(min(1, q_i) ** w_i), with area quality q_area = area_reference / candidate_functional_area. The weights below sum to 1; metric dimensions describe measurements and do not determine weights. Physical, extraction, or functional rejection scores zero; other missing or invalid measurements leave the score unknown, and zero-weight observations must also be valid. Matching source-circuit performance at the reference area scores 100 points; each metric is capped at quality 1, so scores cannot exceed 100. Weights express quality tradeoffs rather than hard bounds.</p>
